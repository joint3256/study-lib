## Unit 1 认识你我·沟通（Knowing Me, Knowing You，人际与沟通）

> 板块：阅读（人际沟通）→ 语法（定语从句入门：who/which/that）→ 写作（应用文：道歉信/感谢信）。
> 话题主线：理解他人、有效沟通、情绪与共情。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：人际交往、沟通技巧、换位思考。
- 功能意念：表达感受、道歉、感谢、建议。
- 语法重点：定语从句（修饰名词，像"长的形容词"）。

```
定语从句入门
├── 先行词：被修饰的名词/代词
├── 关系代词
│   ├── who 指人（主/宾）
│   ├── whom 指人（宾，正式）
│   ├── which 指物
│   ├── that 指人/物
│   └── whose 表"谁的"（所属）
└── 例句：The girl who is standing there is my sister.
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| communicate | v. | 交流 | communicate with sb. | We should communicate with parents. |
| empathy* | n. | 共情，同理心 | show empathy | Empathy helps build trust. |
| conflict | n. | 冲突 | avoid a conflict | We should avoid conflicts. |
| appreciate | v. | 感激；欣赏 | I appreciate your help. | I really appreciate your kindness. |
| misunderstand | v. | 误解 | misunderstand sb. | Don't misunderstand me. |
| forgive | v. | 原谅 | forgive sb. for sth. | Please forgive me for being late. |
| be on good terms with | 短语 | 与…关系好 | = get along well with | I am on good terms with my classmates. |
| put oneself in one's shoes | 短语 | 设身处地 | = put oneself in others' position | Put yourself in her shoes. |
| work out | 短语 | 解决；算出 | work out a problem | We worked out the misunderstanding. |

### 三、语法要点

**定语从句（入门）**
- 通俗：一个普通形容词（a kind girl）太短，要表达"那个对我很好的女孩"，就用一个句子去修饰名词——这就是定语从句。
- 结构：先行词（名词）+ 关系词 + 从句。
- 关系代词选择：
  - 指人作主语：The boy **who/that** won the prize is my friend.
  - 指物作宾语：The book **which/that** I bought is interesting.（作宾语可省略）
  - 表所属：The boy **whose** father is a doctor studies hard.
- 例句：This is the house **which** Lu Xun once lived in.
- 易错：❌ The man which is talking is my teacher.（人不用 which）→ ✅ The man **who/that** is talking…
- 易错：❌ I don't know the man which you talked about. → ✅ …the man **who/whom/that** you talked about（或省略）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（道歉信/感谢信）**
- 道歉：I am terribly sorry for what I did yesterday.
- 解释：The reason is that I was stuck in the traffic.
- 感谢：I am writing to express my sincere thanks to you.
- 收尾：I hope you can accept my apology.

**写作框架（道歉信，约 80 词）**
- 致歉 → 原因（不找借口）→ 补救措施 → 再次请求原谅。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（定语从句关系词）**
- 步骤：① 找先行词是人还是物；② 看从句缺什么成分（主语/宾语/定语）→ 选关系词。
- 例：The girl ______ mother is a teacher studies well. → 缺"谁的"，填 **whose**.

**题型2：完形填空（沟通类）**
- 通法：抓作者情感线，形容词/副词看上下文褒贬。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The man which is here is my dad. → ✅ The man **who/that** is here…
- ❌ I appreciate you to help me. → ✅ I appreciate **your helping** me / I appreciate it that you help me.
- ❌ apologize sb. → ✅ apologize **to** sb. **for** sth.
- ❌ communicate to sb. → ✅ communicate **with** sb.
- ❌ whose 指物错用 → ✅ The house **whose** window is broken = the window **of which** is broken.

## Unit 2 有所作为·公益（Making a Difference，公益与社会责任）

> 板块：阅读（志愿者/公益人物）→ 语法（定语从句：关系副词 when/where/why）→ 写作（人物介绍/倡议书）。
> 话题主线：志愿服务、社会责任、榜样人物。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：公益、志愿活动、社会责任。
- 功能意念：描述人物事迹、发出号召。
- 语法重点：关系副词 when / where / why。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| volunteer | n./v. | 志愿者；自愿做 | volunteer to do | She volunteers at the old people's home. |
| contribute | v. | 贡献，捐助 | contribute to | Everyone should contribute to society. |
| devotion | n. | 奉献 | devotion to | His devotion to teaching moved us. |
| generous | adj. | 慷慨的 | be generous with | He is generous with his money. |
| sacrifice | v./n. | 牺牲 | sacrifice…for… | She sacrificed her time for the children. |
| welfare | n. | 福利；幸福 | social welfare | We care about public welfare. |
| make a contribution to | 短语 | 为…做贡献 | to 是介词 | Make a contribution to society. |
| care about | 短语 | 关心；在乎 | = care for（关心） | We should care about others. |
| be involved in | 短语 | 参与 | = take part in | He is involved in the project. |

### 三、语法要点

**关系副词 when / where / why**
- 通俗：当定语从句不缺主宾，而是缺"时间/地点/原因"状语时，用关系副词。
- when = 介词 + which（表时间）：I still remember the day **when** we first met.
- where = 介词 + which（表地点）：This is the school **where** I studied.
- why = for which（表原因）：Tell me the reason **why** you were late.
- 区分： This is the house **which** we bought.（从句缺宾语，用 which）
         This is the house **where** I live.（从句不缺主宾，live 不及物，用 where）
- 易错：❌ I will never forget the day when I spent in Paris.（spent 缺宾语）→ ✅ …the day **which/that** I spent…（spent sth.，缺宾语用关系代词）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（人物介绍/倡议）**
- 人物：He is a teacher who has devoted most of his life to rural education.
- 事迹：Not only does he teach, but he also helps poor students.
- 号召：Let's join hands and make a difference together.

**写作框架（人物介绍：A role model in my heart，约 100 词）**
- 人物是谁 → 用一两个定语从句写事迹 → 他的精神对我的影响。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（关系副词）**
- 步骤：从句主谓宾齐全 → 看先行词是时间/地点/原因 → 选 when/where/why。
- 例：This is the village ______ I grew up. → 不缺主宾，地点，填 **where**.

**题型2：读后续写（公益/助人情节）**
- 通法：动作链 + 心理描写；结尾升华"帮助他人即快乐自己"。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ the day which I met her（从句不缺宾，缺时间）→ ✅ the day **when** I met her.
- ❌ contribute to do → ✅ contribute to **doing**（to 介词）。
- ❌ devote his time to help → ✅ devote his time to **helping**.
- ❌ generous to money → ✅ generous **with** money / **to** others.

## Unit 3 战争与和平（War and Peace，历史与和平）

> 板块：阅读（战争历史/和平主题）→ 语法（名词性从句入门：宾语从句）→ 写作（议论文：和平的意义）。
> 话题主线：战争历史、反思战争、珍爱和平。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：历史事件、和平与战争、人类命运。
- 功能意念：叙述历史事件、表达观点。
- 语法重点：宾语从句（that/what/if/whether 引导）。

```
名词性从句入门（宾语从句）
├── 作动词/介词宾语
├── that（不充当成分，常省略）：I think (that) he is right.
├── what（=the thing that，充当成分）：I don't know what he wants.
└── if/whether（是否）：I wonder whether/if he will come.
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| peace | n. | 和平 | in peace | We live in peace. |
| war | n. | 战争 | break out a war | The war broke out in 1939. |
| invade | v. | 入侵 | invade a country | The army invaded the city. |
| abandon | v. | 抛弃，放弃 | abandon the plan | They had to abandon the village. |
| suffer | v. | 遭受 | suffer from | Many people suffered from hunger. |
| victory | n. | 胜利 | win a victory | The victory belonged to peace. |
| be at war with | 短语 | 与…交战 | be at war with… | The two countries were at war. |
| break out | 短语 | （战争/火灾）爆发 | 无被动 | The war broke out suddenly. |
| lay down one's arms | 短语 | 放下武器 | The soldiers laid down their arms. |

### 三、语法要点

**宾语从句（名词性从句入门）**
- 通俗：一个句子作动词的宾语——"我知道……"，后面"……"是个完整句子。
- that：引导陈述句宾语从句，口语可省。例：I believe **(that) you are right**.
- what："……的东西/事情"，在从句中充当主/宾/表。例：I don't know **what he said**.（≠ that）
- if/whether："是否"。例：I wonder **whether he will come**.
- 语序：宾语从句一律用**陈述语序**（主语+谓语），不用疑问语序。
- 例句：Can you tell me **where he lives**?（不用 where does he live）
- 易错：❌ I don't know what is he doing. → ✅ I don't know **what he is doing**.（陈述语序）

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（议论文：珍爱和平）**
- 引入：War brings suffering to millions of people.
- 观点：It is our duty to value peace and say no to war.
- 结尾：Only when we cherish peace can we build a better world.

**写作框架（议论文：Peace is priceless，约 100 词）**
- 引出战争危害 → 和平的意义 → 呼吁。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（宾语从句连接词）**
- 步骤：陈述句用 that；"什么/……的"用 what；"是否"用 if/whether。
- 例：I wonder ______ he will agree. → **whether/if**.

**题型2：阅读理解（历史类）**
- 通法：抓时间、地点、人物、事件四要素；注意作者态度词。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I don't know what is wrong with him.（此句 what 作主语，语序本就如此）→ ✅ I don't know what is wrong.（what 是从句主语，语序不倒装）
- ❌ ask sb. sth. / 陈述语序混用 → ✅ Can you tell me **where he works**.
- ❌ The war was broken out in 1939. → ✅ The war **broke out**（break out 无被动）。
- ❌ suffer from the war（一般可）/ suffer hunger → ✅ suffer **from** a disease；suffer hunger（直接接痛苦）。

## Unit 4 未来一瞥·科技（A Glimpse of the Future，科技发展与创新）

> 板块：阅读（未来科技/AI）→ 语法（名词性从句：主语从句初步）→ 写作（议论文：科技利弊）。
> 话题主线：人工智能、未来生活、科技伦理。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会/科技）：AI、机器人、未来生活、科技的双刃剑。
- 功能意念：预测未来、辩证讨论。
- 语法重点：主语从句（It is + adj. + that…）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| technology | n. | 技术 | advanced technology | Technology changes our life. |
| artificial | adj. | 人工的 | artificial intelligence (AI) | AI is developing fast. |
| intelligent | adj. | 智能的，聪明的 | an intelligent robot | The intelligent machine can talk. |
| innovation | n. | 创新 | technological innovation | Innovation drives progress. |
| replace | v. | 取代 | replace A with B | Machines cannot replace humans. |
| convenient | adj. | 方便的 | It is convenient to do. | Online shopping is convenient. |
| have an effect on | 短语 | 对…有影响 | = affect | Mobile phones have an effect on our life. |
| lead to | 短语 | 导致 | = result in | Hard work leads to success. |
| rely on | 短语 | 依赖，依靠 | = depend on | Don't rely too much on technology. |

### 三、语法要点

**主语从句初步（It 作形式主语）**
- 结构：It + be + adj./n. + that 从句（真正主语后置，避免头重脚轻）。
- 例句：**It is important that** we should use technology wisely.
- 另：What he said **is** true.（从句直接作主语，谓语用单数）
- 易错：❌ It is important that we are careful.（that 从句用陈述语气即可；正式虚拟用 should+动原，非考点重点）
- 易错：❌ What he need are time. → ✅ What he **needs** is time.（主语从句作主语，谓语单数）

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科技利弊）**
- 利：Technology makes our life more convenient and efficient.
- 弊：However, it may also make us lazy and lonely.
- 结论：We should be wise masters of technology, not its slaves.

**写作框架（议论文：Does technology make life better，约 100 词）**
- 正方 → 反方 → 我的观点（It is our attitude that matters.）。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（主语从句/连接词）**
- 步骤：句首是句子作主语 → 缺成分用 what，完整用 that。
- 例：______ he wants is a chance. → 从句缺宾语"……的东西"，填 **What**.

**题型2：七选五（科技类说明文）**
- 通法：找代词 it/this 指代、找段落主题句。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ It is convenient for we. → ✅ It is convenient for **us**.
- ❌ invent / 辨析：invent（发明新事物）/ discover（发现已有）。
- ❌ lead to do → ✅ lead to **doing**（to 介词）。
- ❌ What he said are right. → ✅ What he said **is** right.

## Unit 5 自然与文化（Nature and Culture，跨文化沟通）

> 板块：阅读（自然景观中的文化）→ 语法（定语从句综合：介词+which/whom）→ 写作（跨文化介绍）。
> 话题主线：自然遗产、文化遗产、跨文化理解。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：世界遗产、文化多样性、跨文化交流。
- 功能意念：介绍景点/文化、比较不同文化。
- 语法重点：介词 + which/whom。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| heritage* | n. | 遗产 | cultural heritage | The Great Wall is our cultural heritage. |
| preserve | v. | 保护，保存 | preserve traditions | We should preserve old traditions. |
| civilisation | n. | 文明 | ancient civilisation | China has an ancient civilisation. |
| diverse | adj. | 多样的 | diverse cultures | China has diverse cultures. |
| relic | n. | 遗迹，遗物 | cultural relics | These relics tell old stories. |
| attract | v. | 吸引 | attract tourists | The place attracts many visitors. |
| be famous as / for | 短语 | 作为/因…著名 | Hangzhou is famous for the West Lake. |
| date back to | 短语 | 追溯到 | = date from | The temple dates back to the Tang Dynasty. |
| be home to | 短语 | 是…的所在地 | The park is home to rare plants. |

### 三、语法要点

**介词 + which/whom**
- 通俗：定语从句里若动词需要介词（live in, talk about），可把介词提前到关系词前。
- 结构：先行词 + 介词 + which（物）/ whom（人）+ 从句。
- 例句：This is the house **in which** Lu Xun lived. (= which Lu Xun lived in.)
         The man **to whom** I spoke is our teacher. (= who/whom I spoke to)
- 易错：❌ This is the house in where he lives. → ✅ …in **which** he lives（where 前不加介词）。
- 易错：介词后指人只能用 whom，不用 who/that。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（跨文化介绍）**
- 介绍：The West Lake, which lies in Hangzhou, is a famous place of interest.
- 文化：Every culture has its own beauty, and we should respect differences.
- 保护：It is our duty to protect our cultural heritage.

**写作框架（说明文：A place of interest I love，约 100 词）**
- 位置历史（用定语从句）→ 景点特色 → 保护意义。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（介词+which/whom）**
- 步骤：看从句动词搭配需要哪个介词，提前到 which/whom 前。
- 例：The person ______ ______ I spoke is my coach. → 填 **to whom**.

**题型2：阅读理解（文化遗产类）**
- 通法：数字、年代、专有名词定位；注意作者的保护态度。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ in where he lives → ✅ in **which** he lives.
- ❌ date back to the Tang Dynasty 用被动 → ✅ date back to 主动形式表被动含义，无被动。
- ❌ preserve / protect 混用可，注意 preserve 侧重"保存原样"，protect 侧重"免受伤害"。
- ❌ diverse of cultures → ✅ diverse cultures（adj. 直接修饰）。

## Unit 6 太空探索（Space and Beyond，科技发展与创新）

> 板块：阅读（航天探索）→ 语法（定语从句复习 + 名词性从句综合）→ 写作（科普说明文/演讲稿）。
> 话题主线：航天成就、探索精神、人类与宇宙。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会/科技）：航天事业、探索未知、科学精神。
- 功能意念：介绍成就、发表演讲。
- 语法重点：三大从句综合复习（定语从句 + 名词性从句）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| astronaut | n. | 宇航员 | a taikonaut/astronaut | The astronaut walked in space. |
| orbit | n./v. | 轨道；绕轨道 | in orbit | The satellite is in orbit. |
| launch | v./n. | 发射 | launch a rocket | China launched a spaceship. |
| universe | n. | 宇宙 | the universe | The universe is vast. |
| explore | v. | 探索 | explore space | Humans explore the universe. |
| gravity | n. | 重力 | zero gravity | In space, there is almost no gravity. |
| carry out | 短语 | 执行，开展 | = conduct | They carried out experiments. |
| be proud of | 短语 | 为…骄傲 | We are proud of our space achievements. |
| make progress | 短语 | 取得进步 | China has made great progress in space. |

### 三、语法要点

**三大从句综合（本册收尾）**
- 定语从句：修饰名词（先行词 + 关系词）。例：The rocket **which was launched** carries three astronauts.
- 宾语从句：动词后。例：Scientists believe **that we will land on Mars**.
- 主语从句：句首/It 后。例：**It is known to all that** China's space industry has developed rapidly.
- 区分口诀：定语从句修饰名词；名词性从句充当主/宾/表/同位语。
- 易错：❌ The news which he told us is true.（定语从句，which 作宾语）/ The news that our team won is true.（同位语从句，that 不充当成分，解释 news 内容）——初步了解即可。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（演讲稿：中国梦·航天）**
- 开场：Dear teachers and fellow students, today my topic is "Exploring the Sky".
- 成就：China has launched many satellites and sent astronauts into space.
- 号召：As young students, we should study science and dare to dream.

**写作框架（演讲稿，约 100 词）**
- 称呼 → 主题 → 成就与意义 → 呼吁与致谢（Thank you!）。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（从句综合）**
- 步骤：判断空格引导的是定语从句还是名词性从句，再选关系词/连接词。

**题型2：书面表达（演讲稿）**
- 通法：有称呼、有结尾致谢；多用被动介绍成就（was launched）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ launch a rocket to space（一般）→ ✅ launch a rocket **into** space.
- ❌ progress 可数（a progress）→ ✅ progress **不可数**，make progress（不加 a）。
- ❌ carry out an experiment 被动混 → ✅ Experiments **are carried out**.
- ❌ universe 前不加 the → ✅ the universe / space（space 泛指太空零冠词，宇宙 the universe）。

> 本册小结：必修第三册进入**从句世界**——定语从句（关系代词/副词/介词+which）与名词性从句（宾从/主从）入门。这是高中语法的第一个高峰，基础薄弱学生务必先把"先行词 + 关系词"的判断练熟。
