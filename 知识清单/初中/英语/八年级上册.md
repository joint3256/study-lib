## Module 1 旅行与交通（Travel & Transport）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 不规则动词过去式；Unit 2 旅行经历与交通方式。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**旅行与交通**。
- 功能意念：讲述一次旅行、谈论出行方式与花费。
- 语法核心：一般过去时（不规则动词）、序数词、交通方式表达。

```
不规则过去式(go-went,see-saw…) → 讲述过去旅行 → 交通方式 → 花费(spend/take/cost/pay)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| trip | n. | 旅行；行程 | have a school trip |
| travel | v./n. | 旅行 | travel around the world* |
| train | n. | 火车 | by train |
| plane | n. | 飞机 | by plane/air |
| subway | n. | 地铁 | take the subway |
| arrive | v. | 到达 | arrive in/at |
| reach | v. | 到达 | reach the station |
| museum | n. | 博物馆 | visit a museum |
| expensive | adj. | 昂贵的 | expensive tickets |
| cheap | adj. | 便宜的 | cheap but nice |

- 重点短语：by bus/train/plane；take the train（乘火车）；arrive in + 大地方 / at + 小地方；how far（多远）；how long（多久）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 常见不规则动词过去式**
- go→went, see→saw, eat→ate, buy→bought, take→took, have→had, are→were, is/am→was, get→got, meet→met, read→read（拼写同，读音变）。
- 通俗：不规则动词过去式要单独背，不能加 ed。

**2. 交通方式**
- by + 交通工具：by bus/train/plane/bike（不加冠词）。
- take + a/the + 交通工具：take the train = go by train。
- walk = go on foot。

**3. 到达：arrive / reach / get to**
- arrive in + 城市/国家；arrive at + 车站/小地点；reach + 地点（及物，不加介词）；get to + 地点。

**4. 花费：spend / take / cost / pay**
- spend 人 + spend + 时间/钱 + on sth. / (in) doing sth.
- It takes sb. + 时间 + to do sth.
- sth. cost(s) sb. + 钱。
- sb. pay(s) + 钱 + for sth.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — How was your trip? — It was wonderful.
  — How did you go there? — I went by train.
  — How long did it take? — It took about two hours.
- 一次旅行框架：
  > Last summer, I went to Beijing with my parents. We went there by train. We visited the Great Wall and the Palace Museum. The trip took us three days. We had a great time.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（花费动词）**：步骤——先看主语是人还是物，再看后面搭配。
  - 例：I ___ 20 yuan on the book. → 人作主语、on sth.，**spent**。
  - 例：The book ___ 20 yuan. → 物作主语，**cost**。
- **单项选择（到达介词）**：步骤——大地方 in，小地方 at，reach/get to 直接接地点。
- **完形（旅行叙事）**：步骤——按时间顺序，注意动词过去式一致。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I by train to Beijing. → ✅ I **went** to Beijing by train.（by 是介词不能当动词）。
- ❌ arrive to Beijing → ✅ arrive **in** Beijing。
- ❌ It spent me two hours. → ✅ It **took** me two hours.（物作主语用 take）。
- ❌ I pay 20 yuan to the book. → ✅ I paid 20 yuan **for** the book.
- ❌ buyed → ✅ **bought**.

## Module 2 计划与打算（Plans & Future）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 be going to；Unit 2 will 表将来。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**日常活动与作息（未来计划）、节假日与庆祝**。
- 功能意念：谈论将来的计划、预测。
- 语法核心：一般将来时（be going to / will）。

```
be going to + 动词原形(计划) → will + 动词原形(预测/临时决定) → 时间状语 tomorrow/next…
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| future | n. | 将来 | in the future |
| plan | n./v. | 计划 | make a plan |
| picnic | n. | 野餐 | have a picnic |
| picnic | — | — | go for a picnic |
| hope | v. | 希望 | hope to do sth. |
| wish | v./n. | 愿望；祝愿 | wish to do |
| wonderful | adj. | 精彩的 | a wonderful plan |
| during | prep. | 在……期间 | during the holiday |
| sound | v. | 听起来 | sound good |
| else | adv. | 其他的 | what else |

- 重点短语：be going to（打算）；would like to（想要）；look forward to + doing（盼望）。

### 三、语法要点

**一般将来时：be going to 与 will**
- be going to + 动词原形：表示计划、打算，或有迹象预测。
  - I **am going to visit** my uncle tomorrow. / Look at the clouds! It **is going to rain**.
- will + 动词原形：表示将来预测、临时决定、承诺。
  - I think people **will live** to be 100. / — I need help. — I **will help** you.
- 否定：be not going to / won't = will not。疑问：Be + 主语 + going to…? / Will + 主语 + 动词原形?
- 标志：tomorrow, next week, soon, in the future, in + 一段时间（in three days）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What are you going to do tomorrow? — I'm going to have a picnic with my friends.
  — Will people have robots in the future? — Yes, they will.
- 假期计划框架：
  > The summer holiday is coming. I'm going to read some books and visit my grandparents. I'm also going to learn to swim. I think I will have a wonderful holiday.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（将来时标志）**：步骤——看到 tomorrow/next/in the future 用将来时。
  - 例：We ___ (visit) the museum next week. → **are going to visit / will visit**。
- **单项选择（be going to 主谓一致）**：be 随主语变：I am going to; He is going to; They are going to。
- **书面表达（My plan）**：步骤——用 I'm going to… 列举，结尾用 I hope… / I think…

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I going to visit. → ✅ I **am going to** visit.（缺 be）。
- ❌ He will goes. → ✅ He will **go**.（will 后原形）。
- ❌ be going to to do → ✅ be going to + 动词原形。
- ❌ look forward to do → ✅ look forward to **doing**（to 是介词）。
- ❌ will able to → ✅ will **be** able to。

## Module 3 比较人与物（Comparing）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 形容词比较级；Unit 2 副词比较级。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**朋友与周围的人、个人情况**。
- 功能意念：比较两人/两物（A 比 B 更……）。
- 语法核心：形容词/副词比较级。

```
比较级构成(-er/more…) → A + be + 比较级 + than + B → 修饰词(much/a little) → 两者选择
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| tall | adj. | 高的 | taller than |
| short | adj. | 矮的；短的 | shorter than |
| long | adj. | 长的 | longer |
| popular | adj. | 受欢迎的 | more popular |
| important | adj. | 重要的 | more important |
| athletic* | adj. | 健壮的 | more athletic* |
| serious | adj. | 严肃的；认真的 | more serious |
| outgoing | adj. | 外向的 | more outgoing |
| both | pron. | 两者都 | both of us |
| however | adv. | 然而 | However, we are different. |

- 重点短语：be good at（擅长）；be different from（与……不同）；the same as（与……相同）；as…as（和……一样）。

### 三、语法要点

**形容词/副词比较级**
- 构成：
  - 单音节 + er：tall→taller, long→longer。
  - e 结尾 + r：nice→nicer。
  - 辅元辅双写：big→bigger, hot→hotter。
  - 辅音+y 变 ier：easy→easier, funny→funnier。
  - 多音节/部分双音节：more + 原级：more popular, more important。
  - 不规则：good/well→better, bad→worse, many/much→more, little→less。
- 句型：A + be/动词 + 比较级 + **than** + B。
  - Tom is **taller than** Jim.
- 修饰比较级：much（……得多）、a little（一点儿）、even（更……）。much **bigger**。
- as + 原级 + as：和……一样。as tall as。not as/so…as：不如。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Is Tom taller than Jim? — Yes, he is.
  — A friend is like a mirror. / We both enjoy studying.
- 我和朋友框架：
  > My best friend Lucy is similar to me. We are both tall and outgoing. But she is more hard-working than me. I am funnier than her. We help each other.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（比较级标志 than）**：步骤——看到 than 用比较级。
  - 例：This box is ___ (heavy) than that one. → **heavier**。
- **单项选择（修饰词）**：步骤——much/a little/even 后接比较级。
  - 例：This is much ___ (important). → **more important**。
- **完形（人物对比）**：步骤——抓 than、both、however 等对比信号词。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ more taller → ✅ **taller**（比较级不可再加 more）。
- ❌ very taller → ✅ **much** taller（very 不修饰比较级）。
- ❌ He is taller me. → ✅ He is taller **than** me.
- ❌ biger → ✅ **bigger**.
- ❌ as taller as → ✅ as **tall** as（as…as 中间用原级）。

## Module 4 规则与义务（Rules & Obligations）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 must / have to；Unit 2 允许与禁止综合。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**校园生活、社会与文化（规则）**。
- 功能意念：表达"必须/不得不/不必"。
- 语法核心：must / have to / don't have to / can't。

```
must(主观必须) vs have to(客观不得不) → 否定 mustn't(禁止) vs don't have to(不必)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| must | modal v. | 必须；一定 | You must finish it. |
| have to | modal phr. | 不得不 | I have to go now. |
| mustn't | modal v. | 禁止；不准 | You mustn't smoke. |
| traffic | n. | 交通 | traffic rules |
| light | n. | 灯；轻的 | traffic lights |
| cross | v. | 穿过 | cross the road |
| safety | n. | 安全 | road safety |
| obey | v. | 遵守 | obey the rules |
| litter | v./n. | 乱扔垃圾 | Don't litter. |
| polite | adj. | 有礼貌的 | be polite to others |

- 重点短语：be late for（迟到）；keep quiet（保持安静）；wait in line*（排队）；give a seat to（让座）。

### 三、语法要点

**must 与 have to**
- must：说话人主观认为"必须"，没有人称变化。You **must** follow me.
- have to：客观"不得不"，随人称变 has to；过去 had to。He **has to** wear glasses.
- 否定：
  - mustn't = 禁止（不准做）。You **mustn't** play on the road.
  - don't/doesn't have to = 不必（= needn't）。You **don't have to** come so early.
- 问答：Must I go? — Yes, you must. / No, you **needn't / don't have to**.（不用 mustn't）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What are the rules? — We must wear uniforms. We mustn't run in the hallways.
  — Do we have to finish today? — Yes, we do.
- 交通规则框架：
  > We must follow the traffic rules. We must stop at a red light and go at a green light. We mustn't play on the road. We must cross the road carefully. It's important to keep safe.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（mustn't vs don't have to）**：步骤——禁止用 mustn't，不必用 don't have to。
  - 例：You ___ finish it now; you can do it tomorrow. → "不必"，**don't have to**。
- **单项选择（have to 人称变化）**：He/She/It 用 has to。
- **书面表达（Rules）**：步骤——分 must / mustn't / don't have to 三类列。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Must I finish? — No, you mustn't. → ✅ No, you **needn't / don't have to**.（must 的否定回答不是 mustn't）。
- ❌ He have to go. → ✅ He **has to** go.
- ❌ don't have to（不必）= mustn't（禁止）混淆 → ✅ 意思完全不同。
- ❌ must to do → ✅ must + 动词原形。
- ❌ needn't to do → ✅ needn't + 动词原形。

## Module 5 兴趣与爱好（Hobbies）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 动名词；Unit 2 爱好与邀请。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**兴趣与爱好**。
- 功能意念：谈论爱好、发出邀请与应答。
- 语法核心：动名词（doing 作宾语/主语）。

```
爱好词 → enjoy/finish/practice doing → Would you like to…? / Why not do…?
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| hobby | n. | 爱好 | one's hobby |
| collect | v. | 收集 | collect stamps |
| stamp | n. | 邮票 | stamp collection |
| garden | n./v. | 花园；做园艺 | go gardening* |
| rock | n. | 岩石；摇滚乐 | rock music |
| concert | n. | 音乐会 | go to a concert |
| instrument* | n. | 乐器 | play an instrument |
| violin | n. | 小提琴 | play the violin |
| guitar | n. | 吉他 | play the guitar |
| pleasure | n. | 愉快；乐事 | It's a pleasure. |

- 重点短语：enjoy doing（喜欢做）；finish doing（做完）；practice doing（练习）；Would you like to do?（想要做吗）；Why not do?（为什么不呢）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 后接 doing 的动词/短语**
- enjoy, finish, practice, mind, keep, give up, be busy, look forward to, be good at。
  - I enjoy **reading**. / She finished **doing** her homework.
- 通俗：这些动词后面"动作"要变成 doing 形式。

**2. 邀请与建议句型**
- Would you like to + 动词原形？— Yes, I'd love to. / I'd love to, but…
- Why not + 动词原形？= Why don't you + 动词原形？
- How/What about + doing? — What about going swimming?

**3. play 与乐器/球类**
- play + the + 乐器：play the piano/violin。
- play + 球类（不加 the）：play basketball/football。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What's your hobby? — I enjoy collecting stamps.
  — Would you like to come to my party? — Yes, I'd love to.
- 我的爱好框架：
  > My hobby is reading. I enjoy reading storybooks. I read every day. It makes me happy. Sometimes I practice playing the violin. Hobbies make life colorful.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（doing 宾语）**：步骤——看到 enjoy/finish/practice 后用 doing。
  - 例：She practices ___ (speak) English. → **speaking**。
- **单项选择（建议句型）**：Why not + 原形；How about + doing。
  - 例：What about ___ (go) out? → **going**。
- **听力（邀请应答）**：步骤——接受用 I'd love to；拒绝要给理由 but…

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ enjoy to read → ✅ enjoy **reading**。
- ❌ Why not going? → ✅ Why not **go**?
- ❌ play piano → ✅ play **the** piano；play basketball（不加 the）。
- ❌ Would you like go? → ✅ Would you like **to go**?
- ❌ practice to do → ✅ practice **doing**.

## Module 6 自然与环境（Nature & Environment）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 自然词汇与连词；Unit 2 保护环境。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**自然与环境**。
- 功能意念：描述自然、谈论环境问题与保护。
- 语法核心：并列连词 and/but/so/because。

```
自然词 → 连词(and并列/but转折/so结果/because原因) → 环境问题 → 祈使/should提建议
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| nature | n. | 自然 | in nature |
| forest | n. | 森林 | in the forest |
| river | n. | 河流 | a clean river |
| mountain | n. | 高山 | climb a mountain |
| earth | n. | 地球；泥土 | on the earth |
| pollution | n. | 污染 | air/water pollution |
| protect | v. | 保护 | protect the environment |
| recycle | v. | 回收利用 | recycle waste |
| save | v. | 拯救；节约 | save water |
| waste | v./n. | 浪费；废物 | waste paper |

- 重点短语：pick up（捡起）；turn off（关掉）；plant trees（植树）；cut down（砍倒）；play a part in（参与）。

### 三、语法要点

**并列连词 and / but / so / because**
- and（并列，和）：I like English **and** Chinese.
- but（转折，但是）：The room is small, **but** it's clean.
- so（结果，所以）：It was late, **so** I hurried.
- because（原因，因为）：I stayed at home **because** it rained.
- 易错：because 与 so 不能同时用在一句里（选其一）。

**提建议：should / shouldn't**
- We **should** save water. / We **shouldn't** waste paper.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What can we do to protect the earth? — We should save water and plant more trees.
  — We shouldn't throw rubbish everywhere.
- 环保短文框架：
  > The earth is our home, but it is in danger. There is more and more pollution. We should protect it. We should save water, plant trees and recycle waste. We shouldn't cut down too many trees. Let's work together.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（连词逻辑）**：步骤——判断前后句是并列、转折、因果。
  - 例：It was raining, ___ we stayed at home. → 结果，**so**。
  - 例：I like cats, ___ she likes dogs. → 转折，**but**。
- **单项选择（because/so 不同用）**：because 和 so 只留一个。
- **书面表达（Protect the earth）**：步骤——问题→原因→措施（We should/shouldn't）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Because it rained, so we stayed home. → ✅ 保留一个：Because it rained, we stayed home. / It rained, so we stayed home.
- ❌ He is tired, and he is happy.（转折用错）→ ✅ He is tired, **but** he is happy.
- ❌ protect the environment from… 搭配不准 → ✅ protect… **from**…（保护……免受）。
- ❌ should to save → ✅ should **save**.
- ❌ waste water 与 save water 反义混淆 → 记：save 节约，waste 浪费。
