# 应用文写作：模板+高级替换

---

## 方法：应用文"三段式模板+高级词汇替换"法

### ① 方法名
高中应用文写作模板升级法

### ② 适用题型
高考英语应用文写作（15分：书信/通知/演讲稿/倡议书等）

### ③ 原理（语法 form 与意义 use）

**评分逻辑：**
- 要点齐全（漏要点扣分最多）
- 语法正确（每处错误扣0.5-1分）
- 连贯流畅（用连接词）
- 词汇句式有变化（全用简单句得不了高分）

**提分核心：** 不是写多难的句子，而是把简单词换成高级词，把简单句换成复合句。

### ④ 操作步骤

**第一步：审题——确定文体、人称、时态、要点**

**第二步：三段式结构**
- 第一段：开头（打招呼+写信目的）
- 第二段：主体（分2-3个要点展开）
- 第三段：结尾（期盼回复/祝福）

**第三步：高级替换（基础版→升级版）**

| 基础版 | 升级版 |
|--------|--------|
| I think... | From my point of view... / As far as I'm concerned... |
| very good | excellent / outstanding / remarkable |
| many people | a large number of individuals |
| because | due to / owing to / on account of |
| so | therefore / as a result / consequently |
| but | however / nevertheless / on the contrary |
| I hope... | I would be grateful if... / I'm looking forward to... |
| important | vital / crucial / essential |
| help | assist / lend a hand |
| like | be fond of / be keen on |

**第四步：句式升级**
- 加个定语从句：The book is very interesting. → The book which I bought yesterday is very interesting.
- 加个非谓语：Seen from the top, the park is beautiful.
- 用个强调句：It is perseverance that leads to success.

### ⑤ 例题完整演示

**题目：** 假设你是李华，你的英国朋友Chris来信说他最近在学中文，遇到了困难。请给他回信，内容包括：
1. 表示理解和安慰
2. 提出至少两条学习建议
3. 表达祝愿

**范文：**

Dear Chris,

I'm glad to receive your letter. I quite understand that you are having difficulty learning Chinese. Don't worry — it is a common problem for beginners.

Here are some suggestions for you. First of all, it would be beneficial to practice speaking Chinese every day, just as the saying goes, "Practice makes perfect." Besides, reading more Chinese stories can help you improve your vocabulary and feel the language's beauty. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask me.

I hope you will make great progress in your Chinese study. I'm looking forward to your good news.

Yours,
Li Hua

**高级点分析：**
- 高级词汇：have difficulty, beneficial, hesitate, make progress
- 高级句式：it is a common problem... / just as the saying goes... / If you have any questions, don't hesitate to...
- 连接词：First of all, Besides, 逻辑清晰

### ⑥ ❌ 易错提醒
- **要点一定要写全！** 漏一个要点扣2-3分
- **格式分不能丢**：书信要有称呼和落款；通知要有标题Notice
- **不要为了用高级词而用错词**——用对简单词比用错高级词得分高
- **时态要统一**：写建议用一般现在时；写计划可以用将来时
- **字数要够**：一般80-100词，太少扣分
