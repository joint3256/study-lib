## Unit 1 探索与冒险（Exploration and Adventure，人与自我/自然）

> 板块：阅读（探险精神）→ 语法（主谓一致：语法一致）→ 写作（记叙文 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：探险、勇气、探索未知。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：探险精神、人类好奇心、风险与收获。
- 功能意念：叙述冒险、谈勇气。
- 语法重点：主谓一致。

```
主谓一致三原则
├── 语法一致：单数主语 → 单数谓语；复数主语 → 复数谓语
├── 意义一致：集合名词/时间金钱距离等按整体或意义
└── 就近一致：either…neither…not only…or… 靠近的主语决定
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| adventure | n. | 冒险 | have an adventure | The trip was a great adventure. |
| brave | adj. | 勇敢的 | be brave enough to do | He was brave enough to try. |
| explore | v. | 探索 | explore unknown lands | They explored the desert. |
| courage | n. | 勇气 | have the courage to do | She had the courage to speak up. |
| risk | n./v. | 风险；冒险 | take a risk | Don't take unnecessary risks. |
| frontier | n. | 边界；新领域 | push the frontier | Science pushes the frontier. |
| set out | 短语 | 出发；着手 | = set off | They set out at dawn. |
| be willing to | 短语 | 愿意 | = be ready to | He is willing to take risks. |
| in search of | 短语 | 寻找 | They went in search of treasure. |

### 三、语法要点

**主谓一致（form → use）**
- ① 语法一致：Everyone **is** here. / The students **are** working.
- ② 不定代词作主语（everyone/somebody/nothing）→ 单数。例：Everyone **wants** to go.
- ③ 集合名词（family/team/class/audience）：强调整体用单数，强调成员用复数。例：My family **is** big. / My family **are** watching TV.
- ④ 时间/金钱/距离作整体 → 单数：Ten years **is** a long time.
- ⑤ either…or / neither…nor / not only…but also：**就近一致**。例：Neither you nor he **is** wrong.
- 易错：❌ Everyone are here. → ✅ Everyone **is** here.
- 易错：❌ Ten dollars are enough. → ✅ Ten dollars **is** enough.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（探险叙事）**
- 精神：Adventure teaches us courage and independence.
- 经历：With a brave heart, we overcame every difficulty.
- 感悟：The greatest adventure is to discover oneself.

**读后续写（冒险类）高分衔接**
- 紧张：With every step, their hearts beat faster.
- 转折：Just when they were about to give up, a light appeared.
- 升华：It was not the destination, but the journey that mattered.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（主谓一致）**
- 步骤：找真正主语（排除介词短语、插入语）→ 单复数 → 谓语一致。
- 例：Nobody but the students ______ (be) in the room. → 主语 Nobody，填 **is**.

**题型2：读后续写**
- 通法：环境描写渲染紧张，动作链推进，结尾点"勇气/成长"。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Everyone are happy. → ✅ Everyone **is** happy.
- ❌ Neither he nor I are wrong. → ✅ Neither he nor I **am** wrong（就近）。
- ❌ take adventure（不可数泛指）→ ✅ have an adventure / love adventure.
- ❌ set out to do / set about doing 区分：set out **to do**；set **about doing**.

## Unit 2 文明与文化（Civilisation and Culture，人与社会）

> 板块：阅读（文明遗产）→ 语法（主谓一致：就远/就近复习）→ 写作（说明文：介绍文化遗产）。
> 话题主线：文明传承、文化遗产、跨文化理解。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：文明遗产、文化传承、尊重多样。
- 功能意念：介绍文明、谈保护。
- 语法重点：主谓一致综合（with/together with/as well as 就远原则）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| civilisation | n. | 文明 | ancient civilisation | The river gave birth to civilisation. |
| heritage | n. | 遗产 | cultural heritage | Protect our cultural heritage. |
| preserve | v. | 保护，保存 | preserve relics | We preserve old relics. |
| legacy | n. | 遗产，遗留 | a lasting legacy | The Great Wall is a legacy of China. |
| diverse | adj. | 多样的 | diverse cultures | China has diverse cultures. |
| ancestor | n. | 祖先 | our ancestors | Our ancestors left wisdom. |
| be known as | 短语 | 作为…闻名 | She is known as a writer. |
| date back to | 短语 | 追溯到 | The temple dates back to the Ming Dynasty. |
| pass down | 短语 | 传下来 | Tradition is passed down. |

### 三、语法要点

**就远原则（with/together with/as well as/except）**
- 通俗：主语后接 with, together with, as well as, except, but 时，谓语与**真正的第一个主语**一致，不管后面跟着谁。
- 例句：The teacher, together with his students, **is** planting trees.（真正主语 the teacher）
- 例句：Nobody but you **was** late.
- 易错：❌ The teacher with his students are coming. → ✅ The teacher with his students **is** coming.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（文化遗产）**
- 介绍：The temple, which dates back to the Ming Dynasty, is a cultural heritage.
- 保护：It is our duty to preserve our cultural legacy.
- 交流：Understanding different civilisations brings peace.

**写作框架（说明文：A cultural treasure，约 100 词）**
- 是什么/在哪 → 历史 → 价值与保护；用定语从句与主谓一致正确的句子。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（就远原则）**
- 步骤：A with/together with B → 谓语与 A 一致。
- 例：The father, as well as his sons, ______ (like) fishing. → **likes**.

**题型2：概要写作（文化类）**
- 通法：抓遗产 + 价值 + 保护。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The teacher with students are coming. → ✅ …**is** coming.
- ❌ date back to 被动 → ✅ 无被动，主动形式。
- ❌ ancestor 单复数：ancestors 常用复数。
- ❌ heritage 不可数 → ✅ 不加 s.

## Unit 3 文学与人生（Literature and Life，文学艺术）

> 板块：阅读（文学与人生）→ 语法（it 的用法）→ 写作（文学评论 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：文学作品、人生哲理、阅读的意义。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：文学、阅读、人生感悟。
- 功能意念：评论作品、谈阅读。
- 语法重点：it 的用法。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| literature | n. | 文学 | classical literature | I love classical literature. |
| novel | n. | 小说 | a famous novel | The novel is worth reading. |
| character | n. | 人物；性格 | main character | The main character is brave. |
| philosophy | n. | 哲学；人生哲理 | life philosophy | The book tells life philosophy. |
| profound | adj. | 深刻的 | a profound influence | Reading has a profound effect. |
| wisdom | n. | 智慧 | the wisdom of life | Old books hold wisdom. |
| be worth doing | 短语 | 值得做 | The book is worth reading. |
| have an effect on | 短语 | 对…有影响 | = affect | Books have an effect on us. |
| open up | 短语 | 打开；开启 | Reading opens up a new world. |

### 三、语法要点

**it 的用法**
- ① 指代前文事物/婴儿/天气时间距离：**It** is raining. / **It** is 5 km from here.
- ② 作形式主语（真正主语后置 to do / that 从句）：**It** is important **to learn English well**. / **It** is no use **crying** over spilt milk.
- ③ 作形式宾语（think/find/make + it + adj. + to do）：I find **it** easy **to learn** grammar.
- ④ 强调句：**It** was yesterday **that** I met him.（见选必三）
- 易错：❌ I find that to learn English is easy.（可，但更地道用形式宾语）→ I find **it** easy **to learn** English.
- 易错：❌ It is no use to cry. → ✅ It is no use **crying**.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（文学评论）**
- 阅读：Reading good novels is like talking with wise people.
- 影响：It was this book that changed my attitude towards life.
- 建议：We should read more to open up our minds.

**写作框架（文学评论，约 100 词）**
- 作品简介 → 打动我的地方 → 对我的影响；用 it 形式主语/宾语提升句子档次。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（it 作形式主语/宾语）**
- 步骤：句子真正主语/宾语是 to do/从句且后置 → 填 it。
- 例：______ is important to master a foreign language. → **It**.

**题型2：读后续写（文学类）**
- 通法：用文学性语言描写心理与环境，结尾升华哲理。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I find that easy to learn. → ✅ I find **it** easy to learn.
- ❌ It is no use to cry. → ✅ It is no use **crying**.
- ❌ be worth to read → ✅ be worth **reading**（动主动表被动）。
- ❌ have effect on → ✅ have **an** effect **on**.

## Unit 4 自然与可持续未来（Nature and Sustainable Future，环境）

> 板块：阅读（可持续发展）→ 语法（there be 句型）→ 写作（环保议论文 + 概要写作）。
> 话题主线：可持续发展、绿色生活、未来责任。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：可持续发展、绿色低碳、代际责任。
- 功能意念：提出方案、号召行动。
- 语法重点：there be 句型。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sustainable | adj. | 可持续的 | sustainable development | We need sustainable development. |
| renewable | adj. | 可再生的 | renewable energy | Use renewable energy. |
| carbon | n. | 碳 | carbon footprint | Reduce your carbon footprint. |
| recycle | v. | 回收 | recycle waste | Recycle paper and plastic. |
| eco-friendly | adj. | 环保的 | eco-friendly products | Buy eco-friendly products. |
| responsibility | n. | 责任 | a shared responsibility | Protecting the earth is our shared responsibility. |
| there be | 句型 | 有 | There is no doubt that… | There is no need to worry. |
| take action | 短语 | 采取行动 | It is time to take action. |
| make a difference | 短语 | 起作用 | Small acts make a difference. |

### 三、语法要点

**there be 句型**
- 通俗：there be 表示"存在有"，be 的单复数由后面的主语决定（就近）。
- 结构：There + be + 主语 + 地点/时间。
- 例句：**There is** a book and two pens on the desk.（就近 a book）
       **There are** two pens and a book on the desk.（就近 two pens）
- 时态变化：There **was** / There **will be** / There **has been**。
- 固定句型：There is no doubt that…（毫无疑问）/ There is no need to do…（没必要）/ There is no point in doing…。
- 易错：❌ There have a book. → ✅ There **is** a book.（there be 不能与 have 混用）。
- 易错：❌ There are a book on the desk. → ✅ There **is** a book…

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（环保议论）**
- 现状：There is no doubt that our environment is in danger.
- 措施：There are many ways we can live a greener life.
- 号召：There is no time to lose; act now.

**写作框架（环保议论文，约 100 词）**
- 问题 → 措施 → 呼吁；用 there be 固定句型加分。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（there be）**
- 步骤：句首 there，后面的主语单复数决定 be 形式；注意就近与时态。
- 例：There ______ (be) a lot of water in the bottle. → **is**.

**题型2：概要写作（环保类）**
- 通法：抓问题 + 措施 + 呼吁。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ There have many trees. → ✅ There **are** many trees.
- ❌ There is two books. → ✅ There **are** two books.
- ❌ There is no doubt whether… → ✅ There is no doubt **that**…
- ❌ carbon emission 可数/不可数注意：carbon emissions 常用复数。

## Unit 5 科技与人（Science and Humanity，科技与人文）

> 板块：阅读（科技与人文）→ 语法（构词法：前缀/后缀）→ 写作（议论文：科技以人为本）。
> 话题主线：科技与人文、科技伦理、科技服务于人。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会/科技）：科技人文、伦理、人本主义。
- 功能意念：辩证分析、发表主张。
- 语法重点：构词法（前后缀）。

```
构词法（一）
├── 前缀（改变词义，一般不变词性）
│   ├── 否定：un- unhappy; in- incorrect; dis- disagree; im- impossible
│   ├── 再/回：re- reuse, retell
│   └── 互/之间：inter- international
└── 后缀（常改变词性）
    ├── 名词：-tion education; -ment movement; -ness kindness; -er teacher
    ├── 形容词：-ful careful; -less careless; -ous dangerous; -able comfortable
    └── 副词：-ly quickly
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| humanity | n. | 人类；人性 | for the good of humanity | Tech should serve humanity. |
| ethical | adj. | 伦理的 | ethical standards | We need ethical standards. |
| benefit | v./n. | 有益于；好处 | benefit from | We benefit from technology. |
| convenience | n. | 便利 | for convenience | Modern life brings convenience. |
| wisdom | n. | 智慧 | combine wisdom with tech | Combine human wisdom with tech. |
| balance | n./v. | 平衡 | a balance between… | Keep a balance between tech and life. |
| be beneficial to | 短语 | 对…有益 | = be good for | Reading is beneficial to the mind. |
| be based on | 短语 | 基于 | Tech should be based on human needs. |
| in the long run | 短语 | 从长远看 | In the long run, humanity matters. |

### 三、语法要点

**构词法：前缀与后缀**
- 通俗：英语单词像搭积木——加前缀变义，加后缀变性。
- 常见否定前缀：un-（happy→unhappy）、in-/im-（possible→impossible）、dis-（agree→disagree）。
- 常见名词后缀：-tion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -er/or。
- 常见形容词后缀：-ful（充满）、-less（没有，careless）、-ous、-able。
- 应用：语法填空给词根，按句法判断词性再变形。
  - 例：He is a ______ (success) businessman. → 修饰名词，用形容词 **successful**.
  - 例：She answered ______ (confidence). → 修饰动词，用副词 **confidently**.
- 易错：❌ carefulless → ✅ careless；❌ happyly → ✅ happily.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科技人文）**
- 辩证：Technology brings convenience, but humanity should always guide it.
- 观点：In the long run, it is human care, not machines, that makes life warm.
- 结论：We should balance development with human values.

**写作框架（议论文：Tech should serve people，约 100 词）**
- 科技之利 → 人文之忧 → 主张（以人为本）；用派生词汇丰富表达。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（构词法/词性转换）**
- 步骤：先判断空格需要的词性（名/动/形/副），再按前后缀规则变形。
- 例：To our ______ (satisfy), he passed. → 形容词性物主代词后用名词 **satisfaction**.

**题型2：阅读理解（议论类）**
- 通法：抓论点与论据，注意 however/in the long run 后的作者立场。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ success businessman → ✅ **successful** businessman.
- ❌ answered confident → ✅ answered **confidently**.
- ❌ be beneficial for（偶见）→ ✅ be beneficial **to**.
- ❌ unpossible → ✅ **impossible**.

## Unit 6 未来与我（The Future and Me，综合冲刺）

> 板块：复习构词法（转化/合成）→ 语法（构词法收官）→ 写作（读后续写 + 概要写作综合冲刺）。
> 话题主线：综合运用，高考写作冲刺。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：面向未来、综合能力、写作冲刺。
- 功能意念：综合表达。
- 语法重点：构词法（转化、合成）收官。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ambition | n. | 抱负 | realise one's ambition | Work to realise your ambition. |
| potential | n./adj. | 潜力；潜在的 | reach one's potential | Reach your full potential. |
| contribute | v. | 贡献 | contribute to society | Everyone contributes to society. |
| confident | adj. | 自信的 | be confident about | Be confident about the future. |
| sustainable | adj. | 可持续的 | a sustainable future | Build a sustainable future. |
| lifelong | adj. | 终身的 | lifelong learning | Lifelong learning is necessary. |
| as far as I am concerned | 短语 | 就我而言 | = in my opinion | As far as I am concerned, effort counts. |
| last but not least | 短语 | 最后但同样重要 | Last but not least, keep healthy. |
| all in all | 短语 | 总之 | = in a word | All in the all, future is in our hands. |

### 三、语法要点

**构词法：转化与合成**
- 转化：词形不变，词性改变。例：water（n.水）→ water（v.浇水）；book（n.书）→ book（v.预订）。
- 合成：两个词合成新词。例：classroom, homework, underline, outgoing, waterproof。
- 综合应用：高考语法填空"给词根变形"是必考点——先判词性，再变形。
- 例：The ______ (develop) of AI is fast. → 定冠词后用名词 **development**.
- 例：He gave a ______ (confidence) smile. → 修饰名词用形容词 **confident**.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**读后续写高分冲刺模板**
- 第一段（推进）：As the situation grew tense, he took a deep breath and made a decision. Without hesitation, he…
- 第二段（升华）：It was then that he realised… From that day on, … / Only when we… can we…

**概要写作高分模板**
- The passage mainly tells us that… (主题). It also points out that… (要点). In conclusion, … (结论).

**书面表达通用衔接**
- 开头：Nowadays, … / As we all know, …
- 递进：Moreover, / What's more, / Besides,
- 转折：However, / Nevertheless,
- 结尾：In a word, / All in all, / Therefore,

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（综合变形）**
- 通法：① 无提示词→填冠词/介词/连词/代词；② 有提示词→判词性→变形（时态/语态/非谓语/比较级/派生）。

**题型2：读后续写（综合）**
- 通法：读懂原文情绪与冲突 → 两段各 60 词 → 第一段推情节，第二段点主题；检查时态、人称、衔接语块、主题升华。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ All in the all → ✅ All in **all**.
- ❌ As far as I am concern → ✅ As far as I am **concerned**.
- ❌ reach one's potentials → ✅ reach one's **potential**（potential 常不可数）。
- ❌ contribute for society → ✅ contribute **to** society.
