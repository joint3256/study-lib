## Unit 1 成长（Growing Up，个人成长）

> 板块：阅读（成长故事）→ 语法（动词不定式）→ 写作（记叙文 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：童年、成长中的转折点、责任与独立。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：成长经历、心理成熟、独立。
- 功能意念：回忆成长、谈变化。
- 语法重点：动词不定式（to do）。

```
非谓语之一：不定式 to do
├── 作宾语：want/hope/decide/wish/agree + to do
├── 作宾补：ask/tell/want sb. to do
├── 作目的状语：get up early to catch the bus
├── 作定语：something to eat / a lot to do
└── 作主语：To learn English well is important. (= It is important to learn…)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mature | adj. | 成熟的 | a mature attitude | He became mature after the experience. |
| independence | n. | 独立 | independence from | Teenagers desire independence. |
| responsibility | n. | 责任 | a sense of responsibility | Growing up means taking responsibility. |
| innocence | n. | 天真；无罪 | childhood innocence | The photo reminds me of my innocence. |
| cherish | v. | 珍惜 | cherish the memory | I cherish every childhood memory. |
| transform | v. | 转变 | transform…into… | Hardship transformed him. |
| grow up | 短语 | 长大 | Grow up to be a responsible person. |
| be eager to | 短语 | 渴望 | = be eager for | She is eager to learn. |
| look back on | 短语 | 回顾 | = look back at | Looking back on my childhood, I feel warm. |

### 三、语法要点

**动词不定式 to do（form → use）**
- 通俗：不定式是动词的"名词/形容词/副词化身"，不能单独作谓语，前面常带 to。
- ① 作宾语（常见动词后）：want/hope/decide/plan/refuse/agree/promise + to do。
  - 例：I **decided to study** harder.
- ② 作宾补（sb. to do）：ask/tell/expect/want/invite sb. to do。
  - 例：My parents expect me **to go** to a good college.
- ③ 作目的状语：I got up early **to catch** the first bus.（= in order to / so as to）
- ④ 否定：not to do。例：The teacher told us **not to be** late.
- 易错：❌ I hope him to come. → ✅ I hope **(that) he will come**.（hope 不接 sb. to do）。
- 易错：使役动词 make/let/have、感官动词 see/hear 后接不带 to：He made me **laugh**.（被动才还原 to：I was made **to laugh**）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（成长回忆）**
- 回忆：Looking back, I can still remember the day when I made my own decision.
- 变化：Growing up is not only about height, but also about responsibility.
- 感悟：Every struggle helps me become a better person.

**读后续写（成长类）衔接语块**
- 情绪：At first… / Gradually… / Finally…
- 动作：With a mixed feeling, … / Without hesitation, …

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（不定式）**
- 步骤：动词后表"要做的事"且无并列谓语 → 用 to do；ask/tell/want 后宾补用 to do。
- 例：He decided ______ (study) abroad. → **to study**.

**题型2：读后续写**
- 通法：情绪曲线（低→高），两段式；第一段写转变，第二段写结果与感悟。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I hope him to help me. → ✅ I hope **(that) he will help** me.
- ❌ The teacher made us to recite. → ✅ made us **recite**（使役后无 to）。
- ❌ decide studying → ✅ decide **to study**.
- ❌ to learn English well are important. → ✅ To learn English well **is** important（不定式作主语，谓语单数）。

## Unit 2 提升自我（Improving Yourself，个人成长与规划）

> 板块：阅读（自我提升方法）→ 语法（动名词 doing 作名词性成分）→ 写作（议论文：如何成为更好的自己）。
> 话题主线：习惯养成、时间管理、终身学习。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：自我管理、学习方法、自律。
- 功能意念：提出建议、描述习惯。
- 语法重点：动名词（doing 当名词用）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| discipline | n. | 自律；纪律 | self-discipline | Self-discipline leads to progress. |
| efficient | adj. | 高效的 | an efficient way | Reading efficiently saves time. |
| habit | n. | 习惯 | form/develop a habit | Develop a good reading habit. |
| motivate | v. | 激励 | motivate sb. to do | Teachers motivate us to try. |
| strengthen | v. | 加强 | strengthen one's will | Exercise strengthens the body. |
| progress | n./v. | 进步 | make progress | You have made great progress. |
| focus on | 短语 | 专注于 | = concentrate on | Focus on one task at a time. |
| give up | 短语 | 放弃 | Never give up your dream. |
| stick to | 短语 | 坚持 | to 介词 | Stick to good habits. |

### 三、语法要点

**动名词 doing（form → use）**
- 通俗：动名词就是动词加 ing 当"名词"用，可以作主语、宾语、表语。
- ① 作主语：**Reading** opens the mind. (= It is no use crying… 等结构)
- ② 作宾语：
  - 只接动名词的动词：enjoy/finish/practice/mind/suggest/avoid/consider/give up/feel like + doing。
    - 例：I **enjoy reading** novels. / Would you **mind opening** the window?
  - 介词后必用动名词：be good at / be interested in / look forward to / be used to / devote…to + doing。
- ③ 作表语：My hobby is **collecting** stamps.
- 易错：❌ avoid to make mistakes → ✅ avoid **making** mistakes.
- 易错：❌ I look forward to see you. → ✅ look forward to **seeing** you.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（自我提升）**
- 建议：To improve yourself, you should form good habits and manage your time well.
- 方法：Making a daily plan helps us work efficiently.
- 坚持：Rome was not built in a day; keep going.

**写作框架（议论文：How to improve yourself，约 100 词）**
- 问题（many students lack self-discipline）→ 两三条方法（动名词作主语/宾语）→ 结尾。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（动名词）**
- 步骤：介词后、或 enjoy/finish/practice/mind/suggest/avoid 后 → doing。
- 例：She is good at ______ (draw). → **drawing**.

**题型2：概要写作（方法类）**
- 通法：抓方法要点，用动名词短语概括（Reading, planning and reviewing are the three keys.）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ avoid to make → ✅ avoid **making**.
- ❌ look forward to see → ✅ look forward to **seeing**.
- ❌ suggest him to go → ✅ suggest **(that) he (should) go** / suggest **going**.
- ❌ be used to do（被用来做）/ be used to doing（习惯于）→ 注意区分：I am used to **getting** up early.（习惯于）。

## Unit 3 时代变迁（Times Change!，社会变迁与文化）

> 板块：阅读（社会/科技带来的变化）→ 语法（动名词与不定式辨析）→ 写作（今昔对比类记叙文/议论文）。
> 话题主线：时代发展、生活方式变化、传统与现代。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：社会变迁、科技影响、传统与现代。
- 功能意念：对比过去与现在、评论变化。
- 语法重点：接不定式 vs 接动名词意义不同的动词（remember/forget/stop/try/mean）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| century | n. | 世纪 | in the 21st century | Great changes have taken place this century. |
| transform | v. | 改变，转型 | transform into | The town has transformed into a modern city. |
| tradition | n. | 传统 | keep up a tradition | Some traditions are disappearing. |
| convenient | adj. | 方便的 | It is convenient to live here. |
| era | n. | 时代 | the digital era | We live in the digital era. |
| urban | adj. | 城市的 | urban life | Urban life is busy but colourful. |
| take place | 短语 | 发生（无被动） | Great changes have taken place. |
| compared with | 短语 | 与…相比 | Compared with the past, life is better. |
| from time to time | 短语 | 偶尔 | = sometimes | We visit grandma from time to time. |

### 三、语法要点

**接 to do 与 doing 意义不同的高频动词**
- remember to do（记得要做，未做）/ remember doing（记得做过，已做）。
  - 例：Remember **to lock** the door.（去锁）/ I remember **locking** the door.（锁过了）
- forget to do（忘了要做）/ forget doing（做过但忘了）。
- stop to do（停下来去做另一件事）/ stop doing（停下正在做的事）。
  - 例：He stopped **to rest**.（停下去休息）/ He stopped **resting**.（不休息了）
- try to do（努力/设法做）/ try doing（尝试做做看）。
- 易错：❌ Remember calling me tomorrow.（明天要打电话）→ ✅ Remember **to call** me tomorrow.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（今昔对比）**
- 对比：Great changes have taken place in the past thirty years.
- 过去：People used to write letters, but now they chat online.
- 评论：While enjoying convenience, we should not forget our traditions.

**写作框架（今昔对比，约 100 词）**
- 总起变化 → 过去 vs 现在（used to / now）→ 我的看法。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（remember/stop 后 to do/doing）**
- 步骤：先判断动作"做没做/要不要做"，再选 to do 还是 doing。
- 例：Remember ______ (post) the letter for me on your way. → 要做没做，填 **to post**.

**题型2：阅读理解（社会变迁类）**
- 通法：对比处（used to/while/however）常设题。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Remember locking the door before you leave.（提醒去锁）→ ✅ Remember **to lock**…
- ❌ Great changes have been taken place. → ✅ …have **taken place**（无被动）。
- ❌ compare to（比作）/ compare with（与…相比）→ 对比用 compared **with**。
- 中式 ❌ Our life becomes more and more good. → ✅ Our life becomes **better and better**.

## Unit 4 新闻制作（Making News，网络与媒体）

> 板块：阅读（新闻/媒体）→ 语法（动名词复合结构/被动式初步）→ 写作（新闻报道 + 概要写作）。
> 话题主线：新闻媒体、信息真假、媒体素养。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：新闻报道、社交媒体、辨别信息。
- 功能意念：写报道、评论媒体。
- 语法重点：动名词的被动式（being done）与复合结构初步。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| journalist | n. | 记者 | = reporter | The journalist wrote a report. |
| headline | n. | 标题 | a news headline | The headline caught my eye. |
| reliable | adj. | 可靠的 | reliable information | Choose reliable news sources. |
| publish | v. | 出版，发布 | publish an article | The news was published online. |
| audience | n. | 观众，听众 | a wide audience | The programme has a large audience. |
| false | adj. | 错误的；假的 | false information | Don't spread false news. |
| report on | 短语 | 报道 | = cover | She reported on the event. |
| be based on | 短语 | 基于 | The report is based on facts. |
| figure out | 短语 | 弄清楚 | = find out | Figure out whether the news is true. |

### 三、语法要点

**动名词的被动式（being done）**
- 通俗：当动名词的逻辑主语是"被……"时，用 being done。
- 例句：He didn't mind **being left** at home.（被留下）
         I appreciate **being given** such a chance.（被给予）
- 复合结构：Would you mind **my/me opening** the window?（动名词的逻辑主语用形容词性物主代词）
- 易错：❌ He came in without being noticed. ✅（此为正确被动式）；❌ being laughed 的拼写注意双写。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（新闻报道）**
- 导语：On the morning of May 1st, a volunteer activity was held in our community.
- 主体：Over 100 students took part in, cleaning the park and helping the elderly.
- 评论：We should develop the habit of checking information before sharing it.

**写作框架（新闻报道，约 100 词）**
- 导语（时间地点事件）→ 主体（经过）→ 结尾（意义/反应）；多用被动。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（动名词被动）**
- 步骤：介词/动词后，且逻辑主语是动作承受者 → being done。
- 例：The boy was afraid of ______ (punish). → **being punished**.

**题型2：概要写作（新闻类）**
- 通法：导语即核心，压缩成 who/what/why 一句。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ afraid of being punish. → ✅ afraid of **being punished**.
- ❌ mind me to open → ✅ mind **my/me opening**.
- ❌ false news 可数/不可数注意：a piece of false news。
- 中式 ❌ The newspaper publishes everyday. → ✅ The newspaper **comes out** every day.

## Unit 5 脆弱的世界（A Delicate World，环境与可持续发展）

> 板块：阅读（脆弱生态）→ 语法（不定式的被动式 to be done / 完成式初步）→ 写作（环保议论文）。
> 话题主线：脆弱生态、气候危机、生物多样性。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：生态脆弱、保护生物多样性。
- 功能意念：说明问题、提出措施。
- 语法重点：不定式的被动式（to be done）与完成式（to have done）初步。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fragile | adj. | 脆弱的 | a fragile ecosystem | The ecosystem is fragile. |
| ecosystem | n. | 生态系统 | balance the ecosystem | We must protect the ecosystem. |
| biodiversity | n. | 生物多样性 | biodiversity loss | Biodiversity is being lost. |
| threat | n. | 威胁 | a threat to | Pollution is a threat to wildlife. |
| conserve | v. | 保护，节约 | conserve energy | We should conserve water. |
| sustainable | adj. | 可持续的 | sustainable development | We need a sustainable future. |
| be threatened with | 短语 | 受到…威胁 | Many species are threatened with extinction. |
| lead to | 短语 | 导致 | to 介词 | Wasting leads to shortage. |
| take measures | 短语 | 采取措施 | = take action | The government took measures. |

### 三、语法要点

**不定式的被动式与完成式（初步）**
- 被动式 to be done：当不定式的逻辑主语是承受者。
  - 例：The meeting **to be held** tomorrow is important.（将被举行）
- 完成式 to have done：表不定式动作先于谓语。
  - 例：He is said **to have written** a new book.（据说他已写完）
- 例句：There is a lot of work **to be done**.
- 易错：❌ The meeting to hold tomorrow（主动）→ 会议是"被开"，✅ The meeting **to be held** tomorrow.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（环保）**
- 问题：Our planet's ecosystems are fragile and under threat.
- 措施：Measures must be taken to protect biodiversity.
- 号召：What we do today will decide the world of tomorrow.

**写作框架（环保议论文，约 100 词）**
- 问题 → 原因 → 措施（被动）→ 呼吁。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（不定式被动）**
- 步骤：名词后作定语，且名词是"被做"→ to be done。
- 例：The problem ______ (discuss) tomorrow is important. → **to be discussed**.

**题型2：阅读理解（环保类）**
- 通法：措施、影响处常设题；注意作者态度。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The meeting to hold tomorrow. → ✅ The meeting **to be held** tomorrow.
- ❌ be threatened by/with → 受到某种结果威胁用 with：threatened **with** extinction.
- ❌ take measure to do → ✅ take **measures** to do（常用复数）。
- ❌ lead to do → ✅ lead to **doing**.

## Unit 6 太空彼岸（Space and Beyond，科技发展）

> 板块：阅读（深空探索）→ 语法（虚拟语气入门：if 非真实条件句）→ 写作（科幻类读后续写/议论文）。
> 话题主线：深空探索、人类未来、科学想象。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会/科技）：航天、未来、科学探索精神。
- 功能意念：预测未来、发表设想。
- 语法重点：虚拟语气入门（与现在/过去事实相反的 if 条件句）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| universe | n. | 宇宙 | the universe | The universe is full of mysteries. |
| explore | v. | 探索 | explore the universe | Humans explore space. |
| galaxy | n. | 星系 | distant galaxies | There are many galaxies. |
| orbit | v./n. | 绕轨；轨道 | The satellite orbits the earth. |
| frontier | n. | 前沿；边界 | the final frontier | Space is the final frontier. |
| ambitious | adj. | 有雄心的 | an ambitious plan | It is an ambitious project. |
| dream of | 短语 | 梦想 | to 介词 | She dreams of becoming an astronaut. |
| be home to | 短语 | 是…的所在地 | The universe is home to countless stars. |
| carry out | 短语 | 实施 | Experiments are carried out in space. |

### 三、语法要点

**虚拟语气入门（if 非真实条件句）**
- 通俗：说"假如……就好了"——与事实相反的假设，动词要"退一格"。
- 与现在事实相反：
  - 从句：If + 主语 + 过去式（be 用 were）；主句：主语 + would/could + 动原。
  - 例：If I **were** you, I **would take** the chance.（我不可能是你）
- 与过去事实相反：
  - 从句：If + 主语 + had done；主句：主语 + would/could have done。
  - 例：If he **had studied** harder, he **would have passed**.（实际没通过）
- 易错：❌ If I am you, I will go.（真实条件，表可能）→ 表"假设我是你"用虚拟 If I **were** you, I **would** go.
- 与真实条件区分：真实 if（可能发生）用主将从现；虚拟 if（与事实相反）按上表退格。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科幻/畅想）**
- 畅想：If humans could travel to other stars, what would they find?
- 精神：Space exploration shows human curiosity and courage.
- 结语：The sky is not the limit; it is just the beginning.

**读后续写（科幻/未来类）框架**
- 第一段：设定情境（假如……），描写科技环境与人物行动。
- 第二段：解决 + 感悟（科学服务于人类，探索永无止境）。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（虚拟语气）**
- 步骤：判断是真实还是假设；与现在相反从句过去式/主句 would do；与过去相反从句 had done/主句 would have done。
- 例：If I ______ (be) a bird, I would fly to the moon. → **were**.

**题型2：书面表达（畅想类）**
- 通法：用虚拟语气提假设，用一般现在时谈现实意义。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ If I am a bird, I will fly. → ✅ If I **were** a bird, I **would** fly.
- ❌ If he studied hard yesterday, he would pass.（过去虚拟）→ ✅ If he **had studied** hard, he **would have passed**.
- ❌ dream to be → ✅ dream **of being**.
- ❌ universe 前零冠词 → ✅ the universe.

> 本册小结：选必第二册主攻**非谓语动词（不定式 to do / 动名词 doing，含被动式）**与**虚拟语气入门（if 条件句）**。这是高中语法第二个高峰，基础薄弱学生要先分清"谓语动词 vs 非谓语"——一句已有谓语，再出现的动词多用非谓语。
