## Module 1 现在完成时深化（For & Since）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 for/since 与延续性动词；Unit 2 have been to vs have been in 与经历综合。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**个人情况（学习经历）、社会与文化**。
- 功能意念：谈"做某事多久了"。
- 语法核心：现在完成时（延续性用法 for/since）。

```
have/has done + for + 时间段 / since + 时间点 → 瞬间动词→延续性动词转换
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| for | prep. | （达）多久 | for three years |
| since | conj./prep. | 自从 | since 2020 |
| learn | v. | 学习；学会 | have learned |
| live | v. | 居住；生活 | have lived here |
| keep | v. | 保存；借（延续） | have kept the book |
| borrow | v. | 借入（瞬间） | borrow…from |
| join | v. | 加入（瞬间） | join the army |
| die | v. | 死（瞬间） | be dead（延续） |
| marry | v. | 结婚 | be married（延续） |
| close | v./adj. | 关；近的 | be closed（延续） |

- 重点短语：so far（到目前为止）；up to now（直到现在）；in the past/last few years（过去几年里）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. for 与 since**
- for + 时间段：for three years, for two months。
- since + 时间点/从句：since 2020, since I came here。
- I **have lived** here **for** ten years. = I **have lived** here **since** ten years ago.

**2. 瞬间动词不能与 for/since 时间段连用**
- 通俗：borrow, buy, join, die, marry, leave 这些"一瞬间完成"的动作，不能直接跟时间段；要换成对应的"延续状态"。
  - borrow → keep；buy → have；join → be a member of / be in；die → be dead；leave → be away；marry → be married；close → be closed。
  - ❌ I have bought the book for a week. → ✅ I have **had** the book for a week.
  - ❌ He has died for three years. → ✅ He has **been dead** for three years.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — How long have you learned English? — I have learned English for about six years.
  — Since when have you lived here? — Since 2019.
- 经历变化框架：
  > I have changed a lot since I came to this school. I have learned English for six years. I have kept many books. In the past few years, I have made great progress.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（for/since）**：步骤——时间段前用 for，时间点前用 since。
  - 例：I have lived here ___ 2018. → 时间点，**since**。
- **单项选择（瞬间/延续转换）**：步骤——句中有 for + 时间段，必须用延续性动词。
  - 例：He has ___ the army for 5 years. → 用 **been in / been a member of**（不用 joined）。
- **完形（成长变化）**：步骤——抓 over the years、now 等变化信号。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I have bought it for two years. → ✅ I have **had** it for two years.
- ❌ since two years → ✅ **for** two years。
- ❌ He has left for a week. → ✅ He has **been away** for a week.
- ❌ I have joined the club since 2020. → ✅ I have **been in** the club since 2020.
- ❌ for I was ten → ✅ **since** I was ten（从句前用 since）。

## Module 2 被动语态（Passive Voice）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 一般过去时被动；Unit 2 一般现在/一般过去被动综合。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**科普常识与技术、社会与文化（发明、产品）**。
- 功能意念：描述"被……做"，不强调谁做的。
- 语法核心：一般现在/一般过去时被动语态。

```
宾语提前 → be(am/is/are/was/were) + 过去分词 → by sb.(可省)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| produce | v. | 生产 | be produced in |
| grow | v. | 种植；生长 | be grown |
| make | v. | 制造 | be made |
| build | v. | 建造 | be built |
| write | v. | 写 | be written by |
| use | v. | 使用 | be used |
| cover | v. | 覆盖；封面 | be covered with |
| call | v. | 称呼；打电话 | be called |
| speak | v. | 说（语言） | be spoken |
| plant | v./n. | 种植；植物 | be planted |

- 重点短语：be made of/from/in；be used for doing；be known/famous for；be covered with。

### 三、语法要点

**被动语态结构**
- 一般现在：am/is/are + done。English **is spoken** by many people.
- 一般过去：was/were + done。The bridge **was built** in 1990.
- 主动变被动步骤：
  1. 主动宾语提前作被动主语；
  2. 谓语变成 be + 过去分词（be 随新主语时态、人称变化）；
  3. 主动主语放 by 后（可省）。
  - People grow rice in the south. → Rice **is grown** in the south (by people).
- 易错：不及物动词（happen, take place*）没有被动。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Where was it made? — It was made in China.
  — The telephone was invented by Bell.*（过去被动）
- 产品介绍框架：
  > This kind of scarf is made of silk. It is produced in Hangzhou. It is used for keeping warm. It is loved by many people. It is sold in many shops.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（被动判断+时态）**：步骤——先看主语是承受者（被动），再按时间状语定时态选 be。
  - 例：The letter ___ (write) yesterday. → 过去被动，**was written**。
- **单项选择（主动变被动）**：步骤——找宾语提前，be 随新主语。
- **词语运用（给动词）**：步骤——判断主被动，再定时态。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The bridge built in 1990.（缺 be）→ ✅ The bridge **was built** in 1990.
- ❌ The book was wrote by him. → ✅ The book was **written** by him.（过去分词）
- ❌ Great changes were taken place. → ✅ Great changes **took place**.（take place 无被动）。
- ❌ English speaks here. → ✅ English **is spoken** here.
- ❌ The children looked after well.（缺 be）→ ✅ The children **were looked** after well.

## Module 3 状语从句（Adverbial Clauses）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 时间/条件状语从句；Unit 2 原因状语从句。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**日常活动、社会与文化**。
- 功能意念：表达"当……时/如果……/因为……"。
- 语法核心：时间、条件、原因状语从句。

```
时间(when/while/before/after/until/as soon as) → 条件(if/unless) → 原因(because/since/as) → 主将从现
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| when | conj. | 当……时 | when I arrived |
| while | conj. | 当……时（段） | while I was reading |
| before | conj./prep. | 在……之前 | before you leave |
| after | conj./prep. | 在……之后 | after school |
| until | conj. | 直到…… | wait until 5 |
| as soon as | conj. | 一……就…… | as soon as possible |
| if | conj. | 如果；是否 | if it rains |
| unless | conj. | 除非 | unless you hurry |
| because | conj. | 因为 | because of rain |
| as | conj. | 因为；当……时 | as it is late |

- 重点短语：not…until（直到……才）；as soon as（一……就）；because of（因为，后接名词）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 时间/条件状语从句——"主将从现"**
- 通俗：主句用一般将来时，时间/条件从句用一般现在时表将来。
  - I will call you **when** he **comes** back.（从句 comes 不用 will come）
  - **If** it **rains** tomorrow, we **will stay** at home.
  - I will go to the park **unless** it rains.（unless = if not）

**2. 原因状语从句**
- because 语气最强，回答 why；since/as 表"既然/由于"，语气弱。
- because 与 so 不同句（同八上）。
- because of + 名词/代词：We stayed at home **because of** the rain.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — I will tell him as soon as he arrives.
  — If it is fine tomorrow, we will have a picnic.
  — I was late because I missed the bus.
- 计划框架：
  > If the weather is good tomorrow, we will go hiking. We will start as soon as we meet. We won't stop until we reach the top. Because it will be cold, we will wear warm clothes.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（主将从现）**：步骤——if/when 从句用一般现在时，主句用将来时。
  - 例：I will go if it ___ (be) fine. → **is**。
- **单项选择（because/because of）**：接句子用 because，接名词用 because of。
  - 例：___ the heavy rain, we stayed home. → 名词，**Because of**。
- **完形（逻辑连接）**：步骤——判断前后是时间、条件还是因果。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ If it will rain, we will stay. → ✅ If it **rains**, we will stay.
- ❌ I will call you when he will come. → ✅ when he **comes**.
- ❌ Because he was ill, so he didn't come. → ✅ 保留一个连词。
- ❌ because the rain（接名词）→ ✅ **because of** the rain / because it rained。
- ❌ until 句否定误用 → ✅ not…until"直到……才"：I didn't go to bed until 11.

## Module 4 定语从句入门（Relative Clauses: who/which/that）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 who/that 指人；Unit 2 which/that 指物。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**朋友与周围的人、社会与文化**。
- 功能意念：用一句话修饰名词（"……的人/物"）。
- 语法核心：关系代词 who/which/that 引导的定语从句。

```
先行词(人) → who/that + 从句；先行词(物) → which/that + 从句 → 关系代词作宾语可省
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| who | pron. | 指人的关系代词 | the man who is speaking |
| which | pron. | 指物的关系代词 | the book which is on the desk |
| that | pron. | 指人/物的关系代词 | the girl that we know |
| person | n. | 人 | a friendly person |
| hero | n. | 英雄 | a national hero |
| volunteer | n. | 志愿者 | a warm-hearted volunteer |
| building | n. | 建筑物 | the building which stands there |
| gift | n. | 礼物 | the gift that you gave me |
| novel | n. | 小说 | a novel written by Lu Xun* |
| prefer | v. | 更喜欢 | prefer A to B |

- 重点短语：give a hand（帮忙）；get on with（与……相处）；be good at。

### 三、语法要点

**定语从句**
- 通俗：先有一个名词（先行词），再用一个从句补充说明"是……的那个"。
- 先行词指人：用 who/that。
  - The boy **who/that** is standing there is my brother.
- 先行词指物：用 which/that。
  - This is the book **which/that** tells about science.
- 关系代词在从句中作**宾语**时可省略：
  - The letter (which/that) I received was from my friend.
- 易错：关系代词作从句主语时，从句谓语单复数随先行词。
  - The boys who **are** playing are my classmates.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  - I like teachers who are kind and patient.
  - This is the best film that I have ever seen.
- 描述人物框架：
  - My best friend is a person who always helps others. She likes books which are interesting. We will never forget the days that we spent together.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（选关系代词）**：步骤——看先行词：人用 who/that，物用 which/that。
  - 例：The girl ___ is singing is my sister. → 先行词人，**who/that**。
- **单项选择（关系代词作宾语省略）**：步骤——关系代词在从句里不充当主语时可省。
- **写作（用定语从句升级句子）**：步骤——把两句合一：This is the book. I bought it. → This is the book (which/that) I bought.

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The man which is talking is my teacher. → ✅ 指人用 **who/that**。
- ❌ The book who is on the desk is mine. → ✅ 指物用 **which/that**。
- ❌ He is one of the students who is late. → ✅ 先行词 students 复数，从句用 **are**。
- ❌ This is the book which I bought it.（重复宾语）→ ✅ 去掉 it：the book which I bought.
- ❌ that 与 what 混用 → ✅ what 不引导定语从句（无先行词时用 what）。

## Module 5 情态动词表推测（Modal Verbs of Deduction）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 must/might/can't 表推测；Unit 2 综合。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**情感与人际交往、社会与文化**。
- 功能意念：有把握地"一定/可能/不可能"推测。
- 语法核心：must / might / could / can't + 动词原形。

```
肯定推测(一定 must) → 可能(might/could) → 否定推测(不可能 can't)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| must | modal v. | 一定（推测） | must be true |
| might | modal v. | 可能 | might be ours |
| could | modal v. | 可能；can 的过去 | could be |
| can't | modal v. | 不可能 | can't be his |
| whose | pron. | 谁的 | whose bag is this |
| belong | v. | 属于 | belong to sb. |
| hairband* | n. | 发带 | a pink hairband |
| picnic | n. | 野餐 | at the picnic |
| noise | n. | 噪音；响声 | make a noise |
| neighbor | n. | 邻居 | next-door neighbor |

- 重点短语：belong to（属于）；go to the concert；make up（组成/编造）；run away（逃跑）。

### 三、语法要点

**情态动词表推测**
- must + 动词原形：肯定、有把握"一定是"。The light is on. He **must be** at home.
- might/could + 动词原形：把握不大"可能"。It **might** be mine.
- can't + 动词原形：否定、有把握"不可能"。He **can't be** at home; he has gone to Beijing.
- 对正在进行的推测：情态动词 + be doing。He **must be reading** now.
- 注意：否定推测不用 mustn't（那是"禁止"），用 can't。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Whose book is this? — It must be Mary's. It has her name on it.
  — It can't be Tom's. He doesn't like reading.
- 推测短文框架：
  > Look at the bag on the desk. It must be Linda's, because she likes pink. It can't be Jack's, because boys don't use pink bags. It might also be Lucy's. Let's go and ask.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（推测把握程度）**：步骤——肯定有把握用 must，否定有把握用 can't，不确定用 might/could。
  - 例：The light is on. Someone ___ be in the room. → 有把握肯定，**must**。
  - 例：The watch ___ be Tom's; his is black. → 有把握否定，**can't**。
- **完形（推理判断）**：步骤——用线索词判断说话人的把握程度。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ It mustn't be his.（表"不可能"误用）→ ✅ It **can't** be his.（mustn't 是禁止）。
- ❌ He can be at home; I'm not sure.（不确定用 can）→ ✅ He **might/could** be at home.
- ❌ She must is our teacher. → ✅ She must **be** our teacher.（情态动词后原形）。
- ❌ It must belongs to Mary. → ✅ It must **belong** to Mary.
- ❌ must 表推测无过去式（用 must have done 选学）→ 初中掌握 must/might/can't + 原形即可。

## Module 6 社会与文化、节日传统（Society & Culture, Festivals）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 节日与习俗；Unit 2 社会交往礼仪。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**社会与文化、节假日与庆祝**。
- 功能意念：介绍中外节日、谈论社交礼仪。
- 语法核心：本模块综合运用从句与被动、一般现在时说明习俗。

```
节日名称 → 时间/活动/食物/意义 → 被动(are celebrated) → 文化对比
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| festival | n. | 节日 | the Spring Festival |
| celebrate | v. | 庆祝 | celebrate with family |
| tradition | n. | 传统 | a long tradition |
| custom | n. | 风俗 | local customs |
| greet | v. | 问候；打招呼 | greet others |
| bow | v./n. | 鞠躬 | bow to each other |
| kiss | v./n. | 亲吻 | kiss on the cheek* |
| shake | v. | 摇动 | shake hands |
| gift | n. | 礼物 | give gifts |
| relative | n. | 亲戚 | visit relatives |

- 重点短语：get together（聚会）；give best wishes（致以美好祝愿）；be expected to do（被期望/应该做）；in order to（为了）。

### 三、语法要点

**节日介绍常用句式（综合）**
- 一般现在时表习俗：The Spring Festival **comes** in January or February.
- 被动语态：The festival **is celebrated** by all the family.
- 时间/条件从句：When people meet, they greet each other. If you go to a party, you should bring a small gift.
- 礼仪表达：be supposed to do = should do（应该做）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — How do people celebrate the Spring Festival? — They get together and have a big dinner.
  — In China, people are supposed to shake hands when they meet for the first time.
- 节日介绍框架：
  > The Mid-Autumn Festival is an important traditional festival in China. It comes in autumn. Families get together and eat mooncakes. People admire the full moon. It is a festival of love and reunion.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **阅读理解（文化类）**：步骤——抓节日时间、活动、食物、意义四点。
- **单项选择（礼仪情态）**：be supposed to / should 表应该。
- **书面表达（介绍节日）**：步骤——是什么→时间→做什么→意义。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Happy New Year's Day（重复）→ ✅ Happy New Year! / Happy New Year's Day!
- ❌ shake hand → ✅ shake **hands**。
- ❌ In China, people supposed to...（缺 be）→ ✅ people **are supposed to**...
- ❌ celebrate 与 congratulate 混用 → ✅ celebrate 节日/事件，congratulate sb. on sth. 祝贺人。
- ❌ on the first day in the morning → ✅ on the first morning / on the morning of the first day。
