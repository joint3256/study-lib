## Unit 1 幽默与欢笑（Laugh out Loud!，个人情感与人际）

> 板块：阅读（幽默的力量）→ 语法（非限制性定语从句）→ 写作（概要写作入门 + 记叙文：一件趣事）。
> 话题主线：幽默、笑的疗愈作用、生活中的快乐。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：幽默感、情绪调节、乐观心态。
- 功能意念：讲故事、表达情感、评论幽默。
- 语法重点：非限制性定语从句（逗号隔开，补充说明）。

```
定语从句二分
├── 限制性：不用逗号，去掉句意不完整（必须的限定）
│   ├── Students who work hard will pass.（哪些学生？努力的）
└── 非限制性：用逗号，补充额外信息，去掉句意仍完整
    ├── My father, who is a doctor, is very kind.（我爸只有一个，补充说明职业）
    └── 关系词：指人 who/whom，指物 which，不能用 that
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| humour | n. | 幽默 | a sense of humour | He has a good sense of humour. |
| amuse | v. | 逗乐，使开心 | amuse oneself | The joke amused everyone. |
| optimistic | adj. | 乐观的 | be optimistic about | She is optimistic about life. |
| relief | n. | 缓解，宽慰 | to one's relief | To our relief, he was safe. |
| overcome | v. | 克服 | overcome difficulties | We overcame the difficulty together. |
| applause | n. | 掌声 | burst into applause | The audience burst into applause. |
| play a trick on | 短语 | 捉弄，开玩笑 | = play jokes on | Don't play tricks on others. |
| cheer up | 短语 | （使）振作 | Cheer up! | Cheer up; things will improve. |
| make fun of | 短语 | 取笑 | = laugh at | Don't make fun of disabled people. |

### 三、语法要点

**非限制性定语从句（form → use）**
- 通俗：限制性是"缺一不可的限定"，非限制性是"顺便补充一句"，用逗号隔开。
- 例句：
  - 限制：The students **who want to join** should sign up here.（限定哪些学生）
  - 非限制：My English teacher, **who comes from Britain**, is very patient.（补充）
- 关键规则：
  - 指物补充用 which，**不能用 that**；
  - 关系代词在非限制性从句中作宾语**不能省**；
  - 可修饰整个主句：He passed the exam, **which** made his parents happy.（which 指代整件事）
- 易错：❌ My brother, that is a doctor, works in Beijing. → ✅ …, **who** is a doctor…（非限制不用 that）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 谈幽默：Humor is the best medicine; it can drive away sadness.
- 讲故事：The funny thing was that everyone burst out laughing.
- 感悟：A cheerful heart is good medicine.

**概要写作（Summary）入门方法**
- 通俗：用自己的话、三两句把文章"压缩"成骨架，不抄原句、不加个人观点。
- 三步法：① 找每段主题句 → ② 合并同义、删例子细节 → ③ 用连接词串成 30 词左右。
- 常用句型：The passage mainly tells us that… / The author argues that…

**写作框架（读后续写衔接语块）**
- 情绪衔接：To my joy, … / Much to my surprise, …
- 动作衔接：Without thinking, he rushed out. / At that moment, …

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（非限制性定语从句）**
- 步骤：看从句前有没有逗号；有逗号且指物补充 → which，不能 that；指代整句 → which。
- 例：He failed again, ______ made his parents worried. → 逗号后指代整件事，填 **which**.

**题型2：概要写作**
- 通法：只保留论点与结论，删掉例子；一般 1 段 30 词，用自己的话改写。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ My dad, that is tall, likes sports. → ✅ My dad, **who** is tall, likes sports.
- ❌ make a fun of → ✅ make fun of（零冠词）。
- ❌ to our relief 的拼写（relief 不是 releif）→ ✅ re**li**ef。
- 中式 ❌ He laughs happy. → ✅ He laughs **happily**.

## Unit 2 积极向上·成长（Onwards and Upwards，个人成长与规划）

> 板块：阅读（克服困难/成长）→ 语法（表语从句）→ 写作（应用文：申请信/演讲稿 + 概要写作）。
> 话题主线：目标、坚持、挫折与成长。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：人生目标、意志品质、面对挫折。
- 功能意念：表达决心、介绍规划。
- 语法重点：表语从句（在系动词后）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ambition | n. | 雄心，抱负 | achieve one's ambition | Her ambition is to be a doctor. |
| persist | v. | 坚持 | persist in (doing) | If you persist, you will succeed. |
| obstacle | n. | 障碍 | overcome an obstacle | We must overcome every obstacle. |
| perseverance | n. | 毅力 | with perseverance | Success calls for perseverance. |
| determine | v. | 决定；决心 | be determined to do | She is determined to win. |
| pursuit | n. | 追求 | in pursuit of | He is in pursuit of his dream. |
| give up | 短语 | 放弃 | = abandon | Never give up halfway. |
| stick to | 短语 | 坚持（计划/原则） | to 介词 | Stick to your plan. |
| catch up with | 短语 | 赶上 | Work hard to catch up with others. |

### 三、语法要点

**表语从句（form → use）**
- 通俗：表语位置（系动词后）放一个句子，说明主语"是什么/怎样"。
- 结构：主语 + be/系动词 + 从句。
- 例句：
  - The problem is **that we are short of money**.
  - This is **what I want to tell you**.
  - The question is **whether we can finish it on time**.
- 易错：❌ The reason is because… → ✅ The reason is **that**…（reason 后用 that，不用 because）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（申请信/决心）**
- 表达决心：I am determined to achieve my goal no matter what happens.
- 谈品质：Perseverance is the key to success.
- 申请：I am writing to apply for the position of volunteer.

**概要写作训练框架（成长类文章）**
- 抓"问题 → 行动 → 结果/感悟"三段，压缩为：The story tells how sb. overcame a difficulty and finally succeeded, which shows the importance of perseverance.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（表语从句）**
- 步骤：空格在 be/系动词后；完整句子用 that，"……的"用 what，"是否"用 whether。
- 例：The question is ______ we can finish it. → **whether**.

**题型2：读后续写（成长情节）**
- 通法：第一段写"如何坚持/努力"，第二段写"成功 + 感悟"；情绪由低落转积极。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The reason is because he was ill. → ✅ The reason is **that** he was ill.
- ❌ persist to do → ✅ persist **in doing**.
- ❌ stick to do → ✅ stick to **doing**（to 介词）。
- ❌ determine to do（临时）/ be determined to do（状态）注意后者更常用表状态。

## Unit 3 更快更高更强（Faster, Higher, Stronger，体育精神）

> 板块：阅读（奥运/体育精神）→ 语法（同位语从句）→ 写作（报道/演讲稿）。
> 话题主线：奥林匹克精神、竞争与合作、体育的价值。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：体育精神、公平竞争、团队合作。
- 功能意念：报道赛事、评论精神。
- 语法重点：同位语从句（解释名词内容）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| glory | n. | 荣耀，光荣 | win glory for | He won glory for our country. |
| medal | n. | 奖牌 | win a gold medal | She won a gold medal. |
| compete | v. | 竞争 | compete in / against | They compete in the race. |
| honour | n./v. | 荣誉；尊敬 | in honour of | The match is held in honour of him. |
| spirit | n. | 精神 | the Olympic spirit | The Olympic spirit encourages us. |
| strive | v. | 努力，奋斗 | strive for | We strive for excellence. |
| take the lead | 短语 | 领先 | He took the lead in the race. |
| break the record | 短语 | 打破纪录 | She broke the world record. |
| hand in hand | 短语 | 手拉手；密切合作 | Progress and responsibility go hand in hand. |

### 三、语法要点

**同位语从句（form → use）**
- 通俗：跟在 fact, news, hope, belief, truth 等名词后，用一个句子**解释这个名词的具体内容**。
- 结构：名词 + that + 完整句子（that 不充当成分，只起连接作用）。
- 例句：
  - The news **that our team won** made us happy.（that 从句解释 news 内容）
  - I have no idea **where he has gone**.
- 与定语从句区别：
  - 同位语从句：that 不充当成分（the news that he won——won 后不需宾语）。
  - 定语从句：that 充当成分（the news that he told me——told 缺宾语）。
- 易错：❌ The fact which he told us is true.（他告诉我们的事实，定语从句）vs The fact **that** he won the prize is true.（同位语，内容）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（体育精神）**
- 精神：The most important thing is not to win but to take part.
- 合作：We should learn to compete and cooperate at the same time.
- 报道：The Chinese team broke three records in the competition.

**写作框架（赛事报道，约 100 词）**
- 时间地点事件 → 精彩瞬间 → 结果与评价；多用过去时、被动。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（同位语从句）**
- 步骤：空格前是抽象名词（fact/news/hope/belief），空格后句子完整 → that。
- 例：The fact ______ he passed the exam surprised us. → **that**.

**题型2：概要写作（体育类）**
- 通法：抓赛事 + 精神两点，压缩成一句。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The news which our team won（同位语误）→ ✅ The news **that** our team won.
- ❌ win the champion → ✅ win the championship / **become** the champion；"赢得奖牌" win a medal。
- ❌ in the honor of → ✅ in **honour** of（美式 honor，无 u 可接受，统一英式 honour）。
- ❌ compete with the prize → ✅ compete **for** the prize.

## Unit 4 遇见灵感·文艺（Meeting the Muse，文学艺术）

> 板块：阅读（文学/艺术创作）→ 语法（状语从句系统：时间/原因/条件/让步）→ 写作（文学评论 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：文学、艺术、创作灵感。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：文学作品、艺术形式、审美。
- 功能意念：描述作品、谈创作。
- 语法重点：状语从句（连词成网）。

```
状语从句（按意义分）
├── 时间：when/while/as/before/after/until/as soon as
├── 原因：because/since/as/now that
├── 条件：if/unless/as long as（主将从现）
├── 让步：though/although/even if/while（虽然）
├── 目的：so that/in order that
└── 结果：so…that/such…that
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| inspiration | n. | 灵感；鼓舞 | draw inspiration from | Nature is his inspiration. |
| masterpiece | n. | 杰作 | a literary masterpiece | The poem is a masterpiece. |
| vivid | adj. | 生动的 | a vivid description | The writer gives vivid descriptions. |
| emotion | n. | 情感 | mixed emotions | The story is full of emotions. |
| reflect | v. | 反映；反射 | reflect reality | The novel reflects society. |
| appreciate | v. | 欣赏；感激 | appreciate art | We should learn to appreciate poetry. |
| be based on | 短语 | 以…为基础 | The film is based on a novel. |
| be absorbed in | 短语 | 沉浸于 | = be lost in | He is absorbed in the book. |
| have a talent for | 短语 | 有…天赋 | = have a gift for | She has a talent for music. |

### 三、语法要点

**状语从句系统（主将从现）**
- 通俗：用连词把两个句子连成"主从"关系，说明时间/原因/条件等。
- 主将从现：主句将来时，时间/条件从句用现在时表将来。
  - 例句：I will call you as soon as I **arrive**.（不用 will arrive）
  - 例句：If it **rains** tomorrow, we **will cancel** the match.
- 易错：❌ If it will rain tomorrow… → ✅ If it **rains** tomorrow…
- 让步：Though/Although 与 but **不能同用**（同必修二 because/so 规则）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（文学评论）**
- 介绍：The novel, which is set in a small town, tells a moving story.
- 评价：What touches me most is the selfless love between the characters.
- 启示：It teaches me to value family and courage.

**读后续写（文学类）训练框架**
- 两段式：Paragraph 1 承接原文动作与冲突；Paragraph 2 解决冲突 + 升华主题。
- 必做：与原文时态（一般过去时）、人称、情绪一致；用衔接语块（Suddenly, Finally, From then on）。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（状语从句连词）**
- 步骤：判断两句逻辑关系（时间/原因/条件/让步）→ 选连词；条件/时间从句用现在时表将来。
- 例：I will go hiking if it ______ (not rain) tomorrow. → **doesn't rain**.

**题型2：读后续写**
- 通法：① 读懂原文人物情绪与冲突 → ② 两段各约 60 词 → ③ 第一段推情节，第二段点主题。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ If it will rain… → ✅ If it **rains**…
- ❌ Although he is tired, but he goes on. → ✅ 去掉 but（或去掉 Although）。
- ❌ unless = if not，注意：❌ unless he will come → ✅ unless he **comes**.
- ❌ be absorbed in（拼写 absorbed，双 b）。

## Unit 5 揭示自然（Revealing Nature，动物与自然）

> 板块：阅读（自然科学/动物）→ 语法（状语从句：目的/结果/方式）→ 写作（科普说明文 + 概要写作）。
> 话题主线：自然奥秘、科学观察、人与自然。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：动物行为、生态、科学探索。
- 功能意念：解释现象、说明原理。
- 语法重点：目的/结果/方式状语从句。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| observe | v. | 观察；遵守 | observe animals | Scientists observe animal behaviour. |
| behaviour | n. | 行为 | animal behaviour | The behaviour is amazing. |
| adapt | v. | 适应；改编 | adapt to | Animals adapt to the environment. |
| species | n. | 物种（单复同形） | endangered species | Many species are dying out. |
| migrate | v. | 迁徙 | migrate to the south | Birds migrate in winter. |
| survive | v. | 生存 | survive on | Some animals survive on leaves. |
| be adapted to | 短语 | 适应于 | to 介词 | They are adapted to cold weather. |
| in search of | 短语 | 寻找 | = in search for（找） | They went south in search of food. |
| be related to | 短语 | 与…相关 | to 介词 | This is related to climate. |

### 三、语法要点

**目的 / 结果 / 方式状语从句**
- 目的：so that / in order that + 从句（常配 can/could）。例：He gets up early **so that** he can catch the bus.
- 结果：so + adj./adv. + that；such + (a/an) + adj. + n. + that。
  - 例句：It was **so** cold **that** the river froze. = It was **such** a cold day **that** the river froze.
- 方式：as（按照/如同）。例：Do **as** I do.
- 易错：❌ so a cold day → ✅ **such** a cold day（such + 名词短语）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科普说明）**
- 现象：Some animals change their colour so that they can hide themselves.
- 原理：This is because they have adapted to the environment over time.
- 意义：Understanding nature helps us protect it.

**概要写作（科普类）框架**
- 抓"现象 + 原因 + 意义"，压缩为：The passage explains why/how a natural phenomenon happens and calls on us to protect nature.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（so/such…that）**
- 步骤：后接形容词/副词用 so；后接名词短语用 such。
- 例：It was ______ a lovely day that we went out. → **such**.

**题型2：阅读理解（科普类）**
- 通法：定义、原理处常设题；注意实验结论句。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ so a lovely day → ✅ **such** a lovely day.
- ❌ adapt to do → ✅ adapt to **doing / n.**（to 介词）。
- ❌ species 复数加 s（specieses）→ ✅ species 单复同形。
- ❌ in search for（偶见）→ ✅ in search **of**.

## Unit 6 生存（Survival，人与自然）

> 板块：阅读（野外生存/挑战）→ 语法（状语从句综合复习）→ 写作（记叙文 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：生存挑战、意志、团队协作。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：野外生存、极限挑战、互助。
- 功能意念：叙述经历、描写环境。
- 语法重点：状语从句综合（本册收尾）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| survive | v. | 幸存，生存 | survive the disaster | Only three people survived the fire. |
| emergency | n. | 紧急情况 | in an emergency | Call 119 in an emergency. |
| supply | n./v. | 供应；补给 | food supplies | We ran out of supplies. |
| desperate | adj. | 绝望的；拼命的 | be desperate to do | They were desperate for water. |
| rescue | v./n. | 营救 | rescue sb. from | The crew were rescued at last. |
| tough | adj. | 艰难的；坚强的 | a tough journey | It was a tough challenge. |
| run out of | 短语 | 用完（人作主语） | We ran out of water. |
| keep up | 短语 | 跟上；维持 | Keep up your courage. |
| hold on | 短语 | 坚持；别挂断 | Hold on; help is coming. |

### 三、语法要点

**状语从句综合（本册收尾）**
- 口诀：时间 when/while；原因 because；条件 if/unless；让步 though；目的 so that；结果 so…that。
- 重点复习"主将从现"与"though/but 不同用"。
- 例句：**Unless** we work together, we **will not** survive.（主将从现）
- 易错：❌ Unless we will help, they will die. → ✅ Unless we **help**…

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（生存叙事）**
- 困境：We were stuck in the mountains, and our supplies were running out.
- 行动：We kept calm, helped each other, and finally waited for rescue.
- 感悟：In difficult times, courage and teamwork are the keys to survival.

**读后续写（生存类）框架**
- 第一段：绝境中的行动（keep calm, make a plan, help each other）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ run out 的主被动：We ran out of water. / Water ran out.
- ❌ survive from the earthquake → ✅ survive the earthquake（survive 直接接灾难，不加 from）。
- ❌ in emergency → ✅ in **an** emergency.
- ❌ desperate of → ✅ be desperate **for**（渴望）。
