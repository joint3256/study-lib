## Unit 1 饮食与文化（Food for Thought，家庭与文化）

> 板块：阅读（中外饮食文化）→ 语法（过去进行时）→ 写作（说明文：介绍一种家乡食物）。
> 话题主线：食物与文化、饮食习惯、健康饮食。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：各地饮食文化、餐桌礼仪、健康与饮食。
- 功能意念：描述食物、比较不同饮食、表达喜好。
- 语法重点：过去进行时（was/were doing）。

```
饮食话题
├── 文化：地方特色菜、餐桌礼仪、中外差异
├── 健康：均衡饮食 junk food / balanced diet
└── 语法：过去进行时（过去某刻正在做）
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cuisine* | n. | 菜肴；烹饪 | Chinese cuisine | Chinese cuisine is famous worldwide. |
| flavour | n. | 味道，风味 | a strong flavour | The soup has a rich flavour. |
| appetising* | adj. | 开胃的，诱人的 | an appetising smell | The dish looks appetising. |
| balanced | adj. | 均衡的 | a balanced diet | Keep a balanced diet. |
| digest | v. | 消化；理解 | digest food | Vegetables are easy to digest. |
| preference | n. | 偏爱 | have a preference for | She has a preference for sweet food. |
| variety | n. | 多样 | a variety of | The shop sells a variety of foods. |
| be known for | 短语 | 因…闻名 | = be famous for | Sichuan is known for hot food. |
| be harmful to | 短语 | 对…有害 | = do harm to | Too much sugar is harmful to teeth. |
| cut down on | 短语 | 减少 | cut down on sugar | Cut down on junk food. |

### 三、语法要点

**过去进行时（form → use）**
- 结构：was/were + doing。
- 用法：过去某个**时间点/时间段**正在进行的动作；常配 at 8 o'clock last night, at that time, when/while 引导的时间从句。
- 例句：I **was having** dinner at seven yesterday evening.
- 经典句型：I **was watching** TV **when** the phone rang.（长动作用进行时，短动作用一般过去时）
- 易错：❌ When the teacher came in, I talked. → ✅ When the teacher came in, I **was talking**.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 描述：Dumplings are a traditional Chinese food, especially at Spring Festival.
- 建议：To stay healthy, we should cut down on oily food.
- 文化：Food carries the culture of a place.

**写作框架（说明文：My favourite Chinese dish，约 100 词）**
- 是什么 → 怎么做/由来 → 为什么喜欢 → 它代表的文化。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（过去进行时）**
- 步骤：时间是过去某点"正在"→ was/were doing；when + 短暂动作一般过去，while + 长动作进行时。
- 例：While I ______ (cook), the telephone rang. → **was cooking**.

**题型2：阅读理解（文化差异类）**
- 通法：抓对比信号词（while, however, unlike），答案常落在差异点上。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ When I walked in, he watched TV. → ✅ he **was watching** TV.
- ❌ balanced food（错）→ ✅ a balanced **diet**
- ❌ harmful for health → ✅ harmful **to** health
- ❌ variety of food is/are → ✅ a variety of + 复数名词，谓语看中心词：A variety of reasons **are** given.
- 中式英语 ❌ eat porridge → ✅ have porridge（"吃饭"常用 have 不用 eat，更自然）

## Unit 2 节日与庆典（Let's Celebrate!，节日与习俗）

> 板块：阅读（中外节日）→ 语法（被动语态初步）→ 写作（记叙文/报道：一次节日活动）。
> 话题主线：节日由来、庆祝方式、节日意义。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：中外节日、传统习俗、庆典活动。
- 功能意念：描述节日、按顺序叙述活动。
- 语法重点：被动语态（be + done）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| celebrate | v. | 庆祝 | celebrate the Spring Festival | We celebrate the Spring Festival. |
| custom | n. | 习俗 | a local custom | Giving red envelopes is a custom. |
| ceremony | n. | 典礼，仪式 | an opening ceremony | The opening ceremony was great. |
| decorate | v. | 装饰 | decorate…with… | We decorate the house with lanterns. |
| tradition | n. | 传统 | by tradition | It is a tradition to eat mooncakes. |
| feast* | n. | 盛宴 | a wedding feast | They prepared a big feast. |
| in memory of | 短语 | 为纪念 | = in honour of | The festival is in memory of a poet. |
| get together | 短语 | 团聚 | get together with family | Families get together at Mid-Autumn. |
| dress up | 短语 | 盛装打扮 | dress up as… | Children dress up as ghosts at Halloween. |

### 三、语法要点

**被动语态（form → use）**
- 通俗：主动是"谁做了什么"，被动是"什么被谁做了"——当动作的承受者更重要时用被动。
- 结构：be + 过去分词（各时态由 be 变形）：
  - 现在：English **is spoken** here.
  - 过去：The bridge **was built** in 1990.
  - 完成：The work **has been finished**.
  - 情态动词：The work **must be done** today.
- 例句：The cake **was made** by my mum.
- 易错：❌ The book was written by Lu Xun in 1920s.（被动正确，但注意：不及物动词 happen/take place **没有被动**）→ ✅ The accident **happened**（不用 was happened）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 介绍节日：The Spring Festival is the most important festival in China.
- 活动描述：People put up red lanterns and have a big dinner together.
- 意义：It is a time for family reunion.

**写作框架（报道/记叙文：How I spent the Spring Festival，约 100 词）**
- 时间顺序（Before/On the day/At night）写活动 → 结尾谈感受。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（被动语态）**
- 步骤：主语是动作的承受者（物作主语，且没有发出者）→ 用被动 be+done；按时态变 be。
- 例：The bridge ______ (build) last year. → **was built**.

**题型2：书面表达（节日介绍）**
- 通法：要素齐全——时间、由来、活动、意义；多用被动介绍习俗（It is celebrated…）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The accident was happened yesterday. → ✅ The accident **happened**（happen 无被动）。
- ❌ The book is wrote by him. → ✅ is **written**。
- ❌ marry to（被动混）→ ✅ be married **to** / marry sb.
- ❌ celebrate for… → ✅ celebrate…（及物，不加 for）
- ❌ in memory for → ✅ in memory **of**

## Unit 3 运动与健康（On the Move，体育与健康）

> 板块：阅读（运动精神/健身）→ 语法（副词）→ 写作（倡议书/邀请信）。
> 话题主线：体育运动、健康生活、运动精神。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：体育锻炼、健康习惯、运动员精神。
- 功能意念：建议、邀请、描述运动。
- 语法重点：副词（修饰动词/形容词）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fitness | n. | 健康，健身 | physical fitness | Fitness is more important than looks. |
| athlete | n. | 运动员 | a professional athlete | The athlete won a gold medal. |
| champion | n. | 冠军 | the world champion | She became the champion. |
| energy | n. | 精力，能量 | full of energy | Exercise gives you energy. |
| regular | adj. | 有规律的 | regular exercise | Take regular exercise. |
| compete | v. | 竞争，比赛 | compete against/with | Two teams compete for the cup. |
| work out | 短语 | 锻炼；算出 | work out in the gym | I work out three times a week. |
| build up | 短语 | 增强（体质） | build up one's body | Sports build up our bodies. |
| keep fit | 短语 | 保持健康 | = keep healthy | Running helps me keep fit. |

### 三、语法要点

**副词（form → use）**
- 构成：形容词 + ly（quick-quickly, careful-carefully）；不规则：good-well, fast-fast, hard-hard（hardly 是"几乎不"）。
- 用法：修饰动词（run fast）、形容词（very good）、句子（Luckily, he survived）。
- 例句：He runs **quickly**.（修饰 run）
- 易错：❌ She dances beautiful. → ✅ dances **beautifully**.
- 易错：❌ I work hard / I hardly work 区别 → hard 努力地；hardly 几乎不。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 倡议：Doing sports can not only build up our bodies but also cheer us up.
- 邀请：Would you like to join us in the basketball match?
- 坚持：Never give up, and you will succeed.

**写作框架（邀请信/倡议书，约 80 词）**
- 活动时间地点 → 活动内容 → 号召参加。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（形容词/副词）**
- 步骤：修饰名词用形容词；修饰动词/形容词/句子用副词。
- 例：He is a ______ (careful) runner. / He runs ______ (careful). → careful / carefully.

**题型2：阅读理解（励志类）**
- 通法：励志文抓"困难→努力→成功/感悟"结构，主旨常在结尾。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ She dances beautiful. → ✅ beautifully.
- ❌ I work very hardly.（表"努力"）→ ✅ I work very **hard**。（hardly=几乎不）
- ❌ compete with the prize → ✅ compete **for** the prize；compete **against** the other team.
- ❌ build up to health（中式）→ ✅ build up our bodies / keep fit.

## Unit 4 舞台与银幕（Stage and Screen，文学艺术）

> 板块：阅读（戏剧/电影）→ 语法（过去完成时）→ 写作（影评/介绍）。
> 话题主线：戏剧、电影、文学作品与艺术欣赏。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：戏剧电影、文学作品、艺术评论。
- 功能意念：评价作品、描述情节。
- 语法重点：过去完成时（had done）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| drama | n. | 戏剧 | a TV drama | We watched a drama at the theatre. |
| performance | n. | 表演；表现 | give a performance | The performance was perfect. |
| audience | n. | 观众 | a large audience | The audience clapped loudly. |
| adapt | v. | 改编；适应 | adapt…from…；adapt to | The film is adapted from a novel. |
| masterpiece* | n. | 杰作 | a literary masterpiece | The opera is a masterpiece. |
| director | n. | 导演；主任 | a film director | The director won an award. |
| be based on | 短语 | 以…为基础 | be based on a true story | The film is based on a true story. |
| be popular with | 短语 | 受…欢迎 | = be popular among | The movie is popular with students. |
| win an award | 短语 | 获奖 | win an award for… | She won an award for her role. |

### 三、语法要点

**过去完成时（form → use）**
- 通俗：过去的过去——在另一个过去动作之前已经完成的动作。
- 结构：had + 过去分词。
- 例句：When I arrived at the cinema, the film **had started**.（先开场，后我到）
- 标志：by the time, before, by last week，或上下文有两个先后的过去动作。
- 易错：❌ When I arrived, the film started.（可能指电影在我到后才开始）→ 表"已开始"用 had started。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 评价：The film is based on a famous novel and is full of moving moments.
- 推荐：I strongly recommend it to anyone who loves stories.
- 感受：It made me think about friendship and courage.

**写作框架（影评，约 100 词）**
- 简介（片名/原作/类型）→ 情节梗概（不剧透太多）→ 评价与推荐。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（过去完成时）**
- 步骤：找两个过去动作，先发生的用过去完成时。
- 例：By the time we got there, the meeting ______ (begin). → **had begun**.

**题型2：阅读理解（文学类）**
- 通法：注意人物心理与情节转折，推断题找原文依据，不凭感觉。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ When I got there, the film started.（若指早开场）→ ✅ the film **had started**.
- ❌ base on（缺被动）→ ✅ be **based on**
- ❌ adapt with a novel → ✅ be adapted **from** a novel.
- ❌ audience are/is → ✅ audience 强调整体 is，强调成员 are，皆可；后接单数动词常见。
- 中式 ❌ I very like this film. → ✅ I **really like** this film.

## Unit 5 在路上·旅行（On the Road，旅行与交通）

> 板块：阅读（旅行经历）→ 语法（介词短语/地点介词）→ 写作（游记/书信）。
> 话题主线：旅行方式、旅途见闻、行前准备。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：旅行、交通、跨文化见闻。
- 功能意念：描述行程、指路、谈旅行计划。
- 语法重点：介词（时间/地点/交通方式）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| destination | n. | 目的地 | reach a destination | Beijing is our destination. |
| accommodation* | n. | 住宿 | book accommodation | We booked accommodation online. |
| route | n. | 路线 | a bus route | This route leads to the lake. |
| scenery | n. | 风景 | natural scenery | The scenery is breathtaking. |
| luggage* | n. | 行李（不可数） | a piece of luggage | My luggage is too heavy. |
| depart | v. | 出发，离开 | depart for / from | The train departs at 9. |
| set off | 短语 | 出发 | = set out | We set off early in the morning. |
| by means of | 短语 | 借助，用 | by means of transport | We travelled by means of train. |
| be famous for | 短语 | 因…著名 | Hangzhou is famous for the West Lake. |

### 三、语法要点

**介词（时间 / 地点 / 交通）**
- 时间：at + 时刻（at 7）；on + 某天（on Monday）；in + 月/季节/年（in May）。
- 地点：arrive **in** + 大地方（城市），arrive **at** + 小地方（车站）。
- 交通：by bus/train/plane（不加冠词）；on foot。
- 例句：We will arrive **in** Tianjin **on** Monday **by** train.
- 易错：❌ by the train → ✅ by train（by + 交通工具，零冠词）。
- 易错：❌ arrive to Beijing → ✅ arrive **in** Beijing.

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句**
- 行程：We will set off at 8 and arrive at the museum at 10.
- 建议：Book the tickets and accommodation in advance.
- 感受：The journey was tiring but exciting.

**写作框架（游记/给朋友的旅行信，约 100 词）**
- 去哪、怎么去 → 看到什么做了什么 → 感受与下次邀请。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（介词）**
- 步骤：判断是时间、地点还是交通；按 in/on/at 规则填。
- 例：We arrived ______ the station ______ time. → **at / on**.

**题型2：听力（旅行场景）**
- 通法：提前看选项，重点抓时间、地点、交通方式、数字。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ by the train → ✅ by train.
- ❌ arrive to → ✅ arrive **in/at**.
- ❌ in Monday morning → ✅ **on** Monday morning（具体某天早上用 on）。
- ❌ luggages → ✅ luggage **不可数**，一件行李 a piece of luggage.

## Unit 6 地球优先·环保（Earth First，环境与可持续发展）

> 板块：阅读（环境问题）→ 语法（情态动词初步：can/must/should）→ 写作（倡议书/议论文：保护环境）。
> 话题主线：环境污染、可持续发展、个人环保行动。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：污染、气候、节能减排、可持续发展。
- 功能意念：提出问题、给出建议、表达责任。
- 语法重点：情态动词（can/must/should/may）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pollution | n. | 污染 | air/water pollution | Air pollution is a serious problem. |
| environment | n. | 环境 | protect the environment | We must protect the environment. |
| recycle | v. | 回收利用 | recycle waste paper | We recycle glass and paper. |
| renewable | adj. | 可再生的 | renewable energy | Solar power is renewable energy. |
| carbon | n. | 碳 | carbon emissions | We should cut carbon emissions. |
| sustainable | adj. | 可持续的 | sustainable development | We need sustainable development. |
| take action | 短语 | 采取行动 | take action to do | It's time to take action. |
| run out (of) | 短语 | 用完，耗尽 | = use up | Fossil fuels will run out. |
| make a difference | 短语 | 起作用 | Small steps make a difference. |

### 三、语法要点

**情态动词（form → use）**
- 特点：后接动词原形，无人称变化；表能力/许可/必须/建议/推测。
- can（能力/可能）：She **can** swim.
- must（必须/肯定推测）：You **must** finish it today. / He **must** be at home.
- should（建议/应该）：We **should** save water.
- may（可能/许可）：It **may** rain. / May I come in?
- 易错：❌ He can swims. → ✅ can **swim**（情态动词后原形）。
- 易错：❌ You must to go. → ✅ must **go**（情态动词不加 to）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（议论文/倡议）**
- 问题：Our environment is being polluted, and resources are running out.
- 措施：We should recycle waste and use renewable energy.
- 号召：Every small action can make a difference.

**写作框架（议论文：How to protect our earth，约 100 词）**
- 提出问题 → 两三条建议（should/had better）→ 呼吁。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（情态动词/被动）**
- 步骤：表建议用 should，必须用 must，能力用 can；后接动词原形。
- 例：We ______ (must not) waste water. → **mustn't / must not** waste.

**题型2：书面表达（环保倡议）**
- 通法：问题—措施—呼吁三段式，多用被动与情态动词。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ He can swims. → ✅ He can **swim**.
- ❌ You must to finish. → ✅ You must **finish**.
- ❌ pollutions（可数错）→ ✅ pollution **不可数**。
- ❌ run out of 的主语：We **ran out of** water. / Water **ran out**.（物作主语用 run out，人作宾语用 run out of）
- 中式 ❌ protect the environment is important → ✅ **Protecting** the environment is important（动名词作主语）。
