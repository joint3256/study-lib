## Module 1 日常活动与作息（Daily Activities）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 日常动作动词；Unit 2 一般现在时与频度副词。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**日常活动与作息**。
- 功能意念：说"我经常/每天做什么"，第三人称单数变化。
- 语法核心：一般现在时（实义动词）、频度副词。

```
动作动词 → 一般现在时(动词原形/三单) → 频度副词(always/often/usually) → Do/Does…?
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| get up | phr. | 起床 | I get up at six. |
| brush | v. | 刷 | brush teeth 刷牙 |
| wash | v. | 洗 | wash hands/face |
| have | v. | 吃；喝；有 | have breakfast |
| walk | v./n. | 走；散步 | walk to school |
| ride | v. | 骑 | ride a bike |
| clean | v./adj. | 打扫；干净的 | clean the room |
| often | adv. | 经常 | I often read books. |
| usually | adv. | 通常 | usually go to school by bus |
| never | adv. | 从不 | never be late |

- 重点短语：do (one's) homework（做作业）；go to bed（睡觉）；by bus/bike（乘公交/骑车）；take a shower（淋浴）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 一般现在时（实义动词）**
- 通俗：表示经常、反复发生的动作，或客观事实。
- 主语是 I/you/we/they：动词用原形。I **get** up at six.
- 主语是第三人称单数（he/she/it/单数名词）：动词加 -s/-es。He **gets** up at six.
- 三单变化：一般加 s；以 s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾加 es（go→goes, watch→watches）；辅音+y 变 ies（study→studies）。
- 否定：don't/doesn't + 动词原形。He **doesn't like** apples.
- 疑问：Do/Does + 主语 + 动词原形？**Does** she **walk** to school?

**2. 频度副词**
- 位置：be 动词后、实义动词前。always（总是100%）> usually（通常）> often（经常）> sometimes（有时）> never（从不）。
- She is **always** happy. / He **often** plays football.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What time do you usually get up? — I usually get up at six thirty.
  — How do you go to school? — I go to school by bike.
- 我的一天框架：
  > I usually get up at six thirty. I brush my teeth and wash my face. Then I have breakfast. I go to school by bike. I never go to bed late. I think it's a good habit.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（三单动词）**：步骤——先找主语，第三人称单数动词加 s/es；否定疑问用了 doesn't/Does，后面动词还原。
  - 例：He ___ (go) to school by bus. → 三单 **goes**。
  - 例：Does he ___ (go) by bus? → 前面有 Does，用原形 **go**。
- **听力（听日常活动）**：步骤——抓频度副词和交通方式词。
- **书面表达（My day）**：步骤——按时间顺序写，用 first/then/after that/finally 连接。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ He don't like apples. → ✅ He **doesn't like** apples.
- ❌ She often walk to school. → ✅ She often **walks** to school.（三单）
- ❌ Does she goes? → ✅ Does she **go**?（Does 后动词还原）
- ❌ I go to school by a bike. → ✅ by bike（by + 交通工具，不加冠词）。
- ❌ He always is late. → ✅ He **is always** late.（频度副词放 be 后）。

## Module 2 现在正在做什么（Actions Happening Now）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 动词-ing 形式；Unit 2 现在进行时。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**日常活动与作息（进行中的动作）**。
- 功能意念：描述"此刻正在发生的事"（What are you doing?）。
- 语法核心：现在进行时（be + doing）。

```
动词-ing → be(am/is/are) + doing → What are you doing? → 标志词 now/right now/Listen!
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| run | v. | 跑 | running |
| swim | v. | 游泳 | swimming |
| read | v. | 读；看 | read a book |
| write | v. | 写 | writing |
| sit | v. | 坐 | sitting |
| stand | v. | 站 | standing |
| make | v. | 制作；使 | making a cake |
| use | v. | 使用 | using the computer |
| movie | n. | 电影 | go to the movies* |
| pool | n. | 游泳池 | at the pool |

- 重点短语：talk on the phone（打电话）；watch TV（看电视）；wait for（等待）；look at（看……）。

### 三、语法要点

**现在进行时**
- 结构：am/is/are + 动词-ing。
- 通俗：表示说话时"正在做"的动作。标志词：now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!。
- I **am reading** a book. / He **is running**. / They **are playing** football.
- 否定：be + not + doing。He isn't running.
- 疑问：Be + 主语 + doing？**Are** you **reading**?
- -ing 构成：一般加 ing（read→reading）；去 e 加 ing（write→writing, make→making）；双写末尾辅音加 ing（run→running, swim→swim**ming**, sit→sitting）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What are you doing? — I'm watching TV.
  — What is he doing? — He is doing his homework.
- 描述"公园里的周末"框架：
  > It's a sunny Sunday. Some people are in the park. Look! A boy is running. Two girls are reading under the tree. Others are taking photos. Everyone is having a good time.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（时态判断）**：步骤——看到 now/Look!/Listen! 立刻用现在进行时。
  - 例：Listen! The bird ___. → **is singing**。
- **词形变化（-ing 双写）**：步骤——重读闭音节、辅元辅结尾双写：run→running, swim→swimming, sit→sitting, put→putting。
- **看图写话**：步骤——每句用 be + doing，注意主语与 be 的搭配。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ He runing. → ✅ He **is running**.（缺 be，且双写 n）。
- ❌ She is writeing. → ✅ She is **writing**.（去 e）。
- ❌ I am swiming. → ✅ I am **swimming**.（双写 m）。
- ❌ Look! He plays football.（看到 Look 用一般现在时）→ ✅ Look! He **is playing** football.
- ❌ What are you do? → ✅ What are you **doing**?

## Module 3 方位与指路（Places & Directions）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 地点介词；Unit 2 there be 句型与问路。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**校园生活、旅行与交通（问路）**。
- 功能意念：描述位置（Where is…?）、问路指路。
- 语法核心：方位介词、there be 句型。

```
地点词 → 方位介词(in/on/under/behind/next to) → There be… → 问路(Is there a…?)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| place | n. | 地方 | a quiet place |
| library | n. | 图书馆 | in the library |
| restaurant | n. | 餐馆 | a good restaurant |
| bank | n. | 银行 | near the bank |
| hospital | n. | 医院 | next to the hospital |
| street | n. | 街道 | on Center Street |
| behind | prep. | 在……后面 | behind the door |
| between | prep. | 在……之间 | between A and B |
| near | prep. | 在……附近 | near my home |
| across | prep./adv. | 穿过；在对面 | across from |

- 重点短语：in front of（在……前面）；next to（紧挨着）；far from（离……远）；turn left/right（左/右转）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 方位介词**
- in（在……里）、on（在……上）、under（在……下）、behind（在后面）、next to（紧挨着）、between…and…（在两者之间）、in front of（在前面）、across from（在对面）。

**2. There be 句型**
- 通俗：表示"某地有某物"。结构：There + is/are + 某物 + 某地。
- 就近原则：be 的单复数看紧跟它的第一个名词。There **is** a book and two pens on the desk.（第一个 a book 用 is）/ There **are** two pens and a book.（第一个 two pens 用 are）。
- 否定：There isn't / There aren't。疑问：Is there…? / Are there…?

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Is there a bank near here? — Yes, there is. It's on Center Street.
  — Where is the library? — It's next to the hospital.
  — How can I get there? — Go straight and turn left.
- 描述"我的社区"框架：
  > This is my neighbourhood. There is a library near my home. Across from the library is a park. Between the park and the bank is a small restaurant. I like my neighbourhood.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（there be 就近原则）**：步骤——看 be 后面第一个名词的单复数。
  - 例：There ___ some milk and apples. → 第一个 milk 不可数，**is**。
- **单项选择（方位介词）**：步骤——看图判断位置关系，选对应介词。
- **听力（问路）**：步骤——抓街道名、左转右转、地点词。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ There are a book on the desk. → ✅ There **is** a book.
- ❌ There is two pens. → ✅ There **are** two pens.
- ❌ in the front of（在外部前面）与 in front of（内部）混淆 → ✅ in front of 指外部前面，in the front of 指内部前部。
- ❌ between the park → ✅ between A **and** B。
- ❌ Where there is a bank? → ✅ **Is there** a bank?

## Module 4 能力与许可（Ability）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 情态动词 can；Unit 2 祈使句与规则。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**校园生活（校规）、兴趣与爱好（会做什么）**。
- 功能意念：说"会/能做什么"、给出命令与规则。
- 语法核心：情态动词 can、祈使句。

```
can(会/能) → Can you swim? Yes,I can/No,I can't → 祈使句(原形开头/Don't…) → 规则
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| can | modal v. | 能；会；可以 | I can swim. |
| dance | v. | 跳舞 | dance well |
| sing | v. | 唱歌 | sing songs |
| draw | v. | 画画 | draw pictures |
| speak | v. | 说（语言） | speak English |
| rule | n. | 规则 | school rules |
| fight | v. | 打架 | fight with others |
| run | v. | 跑 | run in the hallways* |
| quiet | adj. | 安静的 | be quiet |
| wear | v. | 穿；戴 | wear the school uniform |

- 重点短语：listen to（听）；arrive/be late for（迟到）；have to（必须）；on time（准时）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 情态动词 can**
- 结构：can + 动词原形（无人称和数的变化）。
- 表能力：I **can** swim. / She can't dance.
- 表请求许可：**Can** I use your pen? — Yes, you can. / No, you can't.
- 疑问：Can + 主语 + 动词原形？简答用 can/can't。
- 易错：can 后面永远接动词原形，不加 to，不加 s。

**2. 祈使句**
- 通俗：表示命令、请求、建议，省略主语 you，动词原形开头。
- 肯定：Stand up. / Open the door.
- 否定：Don't + 动词原形。**Don't run** in the hallways. / No smoking*.
- Let's + 动词原形：Let's go.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Can you swim? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
  — What can you do? — I can play the guitar.
  — Don't eat in class. / We must be on time.
- 校规短文框架：
  > We have many rules at school. We must wear the school uniform on Monday. We can't run in the hallways. We must be quiet in the library. Don't arrive late for class. We must follow the rules.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（can 后接原形）**：步骤——can/can't 后一定是动词原形。
  - 例：She can ___ (swims). → **swim**。
- **单项选择（祈使句否定）**：步骤——否定祈使句用 Don't + 原形。
  - 例：___ (not run) in the classroom. → **Don't run**。
- **书面表达（Rules）**：步骤——用 We must… / We can't… / Don't… 列举规则。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ She can sings. → ✅ She can **sing**.
- ❌ Can you to swim? → ✅ Can you **swim**?（can 后不加 to）。
- ❌ Don't running. → ✅ **Don't run**.
- ❌ No run. → ✅ **No running.** / Don't run.（No + doing）。
- ❌ He can plays basketball. → ✅ He can **play** basketball.

## Module 5 节假日与过去的周末（Holidays & the Past）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 be 动词过去式；Unit 2 规则动词过去式。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**节假日与庆祝、日常活动（回忆过去）**。
- 功能意念：讲述"昨天/上周末做了什么"。
- 语法核心：一般过去时入门（was/were、规则动词过去式）。

```
时间状语(yesterday/last week) → be过去式(was/were) → 规则动词过去式(-ed) → Did…?
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| yesterday | n./adv. | 昨天 | yesterday morning |
| last | adj. | 上一个；最后的 | last week/weekend |
| was | v. | am/is 的过去式 | I was at home. |
| were | v. | are 的过去式 | They were happy. |
| visit | v. | 参观；拜访 | visit my grandparents |
| stay | v. | 停留；待 | stay at home |
| watch | v. | 观看 | watched TV |
| clean | v. | 打扫 | cleaned the room |
| weekend | n. | 周末 | on the weekend |
| beach | n. | 海滩 | go to the beach |

- 重点短语：go to the cinema（看电影）；have a good time（玩得开心）；last night（昨晚）；the day before yesterday*（前天）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. be 动词过去式 was/were**
- I/He/She/It + was；You/We/They + were。
- I **was** at home yesterday. / They **were** in the park.
- 否定：wasn't / weren't。疑问：Was/Were 提前。

**2. 规则动词过去式（+ed）**
- 一般加 ed：watch→watched, clean→cleaned。
- e 结尾加 d：live→lived, use→used。
- 辅音+y，变 y 为 i 加 ed：study→studied, carry→carried。
- 重读闭音节双写：stop→stopped, shop→shopped。
- 否定疑问：用 didn't + 动词原形。He **didn't watch** TV. / **Did** you **stay** at home?

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — How was your weekend? — It was great/good/OK.
  — What did you do? — I visited my grandparents.
  — Did you go to the beach? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
- 上个周末框架：
  > Last weekend was busy but happy. On Saturday morning, I cleaned my room. In the afternoon, I visited my grandparents. On Sunday, I did my homework and watched TV. I had a good time.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（过去时间标志）**：步骤——看到 yesterday/last… 用一般过去时。
  - 例：I ___ (stay) at home yesterday. → **stayed**。
- **词形变化（过去式）**：步骤——按规则加 ed，注意 y 变 i、双写。
  - 例：He ___ (study) English last night. → **studied**。
- **听力（回忆周末）**：步骤——抓活动动词和时间。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I was go to park. → ✅ I **went to** the park.（go 的过去式 went，be 与实义动词不连用）。
- ❌ He didn't went. → ✅ He didn't **go**.（didn't 后还原）。
- ❌ Did you watched? → ✅ Did you **watch**?
- ❌ I were at home. → ✅ I **was** at home.
- ❌ studyed → ✅ **studied**。

## Module 6 天气与喜好（Weather & Likes）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 天气表达；Unit 2 谈论喜好与感受。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**天气、兴趣与爱好**。
- 功能意念：问天气（How's the weather?）、说感受与喜好。
- 语法核心：感叹句入门、like doing/to do。

```
天气词(sunny/rainy/windy) → How's the weather? → 感受(It's+adj.) → like doing
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| weather | n. | 天气 | How's the weather? |
| sunny | adj. | 晴朗的 | a sunny day |
| rainy | adj. | 下雨的 | rainy days |
| windy | adj. | 有风的 | windy weather |
| cloudy | adj. | 多云的 | cloudy skies |
| snow | n./v. | 雪；下雪 | It's snowing. |
| rain | n./v. | 雨；下雨 | rain hard* |
| warm/cool | adj. | 温暖的/凉爽的 | warm spring |
| cold/hot | adj. | 冷的/热的 | hot summer |
| terrible | adj. | 糟糕的 | terrible weather |

- 重点短语：take a photo（拍照）；have a good time；right now（现在）；summer vacation*（暑假）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 天气表达**
- How's the weather? = What's the weather like? — It's sunny/rainy/windy.
- It's + 形容词（sunny, rainy）。It's raining/snowing.（进行时表正在下）。

**2. like doing sth.**
- 通俗：like 后常接动名词 doing，表示长期爱好。
- I like reading. / She likes swimming.
- 常见后接 doing 的动词：enjoy, finish, practice。

**3. What 感叹句入门**
- What + a/an + 形容词 + 单数名词！**What** a fine day (it is)!
- How + 形容词/副词！**How** fine it is!

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — How's the weather in Beijing? — It's sunny and warm.
  — What are you doing? — I'm visiting my aunt.
- 天气短文框架：
  > The weather is different in different seasons. It's warm in spring. It's hot in summer. It's cool in autumn. It's cold in winter. I like sunny days best.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（问天气句型）**：步骤——How's the weather? = What's the weather **like**?
  - 例：What's the weather ___ today? — It's rainy. → **like**。
- **单项选择（like doing）**：步骤——like 后接 doing。
  - 例：She likes ___ (draw). → **drawing**。
- **听力（天气预报）**：步骤——抓城市 + 天气词。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ How's the weather like? → ✅ How's the weather? = What's the weather **like**?（两句不混）。
- ❌ It's rain. → ✅ It's **rainy** / It's **raining**.
- ❌ I like swim. → ✅ I like **swimming**.
- ❌ What fine day! → ✅ What **a** fine day!（单数可数要 a）。
- ❌ It's snow. → ✅ It's **snowy** / It's **snowing**.
