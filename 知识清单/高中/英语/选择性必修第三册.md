## Unit 1 外貌与价值（Face Values，个人审美与自我认知）

> 板块：阅读（外貌与内在美）→ 语法（过去分词作定语/表语）→ 写作（议论文：内在美更重要）。
> 话题主线：外表与内在、审美、自信。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自我）：外貌评价、内在美、自信与自我接纳。
- 功能意念：描述人物、表达观点。
- 语法重点：过去分词 done 作定语/表语。

```
非谓语之分词（一）：过去分词 done
├── 含义：被动 + 完成（被…的）
├── 作定语：a broken cup（= a cup which is broken）
│          The book written by Lu Xun is popular.
├── 作表语：be + done（表状态）：The door is locked. / I am interested in…
└── 作宾补：have sth. done（让别人做）：I had my hair cut.
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| appearance | n. | 外貌；出现 | judge by appearance | Don't judge a person by appearance. |
| inner | adj. | 内在的 | inner beauty | Inner beauty matters more. |
| elegant | adj. | 优雅的 | an elegant lady | She has an elegant manner. |
| confident | adj. | 自信的 | be confident in | Be confident in yourself. |
| prejudice | n. | 偏见 | prejudice against | We should have no prejudice. |
| charm | n./v. | 魅力；吸引 | natural charm | Her smile has great charm. |
| be based on | 短语 | 基于 | True beauty is based on character. |
| be proud of | 短语 | 为…自豪 | Be proud of who you are. |
| attach importance to | 短语 | 重视 | to 介词 | Don't attach too much importance to looks. |

### 三、语法要点

**过去分词 done 作定语/表语**
- 通俗：done 表示"被……、已经……"，常含被动/完成意味。
- 作前置定语：a **broken** glass, **fallen** leaves, a **cooked** meal。
- 作后置定语：The book **written by the teacher** is useful.（= which was written）
- 作表语：The window **is broken**. / We **were moved** by the story.
- 与现在分词区别：
  - developing countries（发展中国家，主动/进行）/ developed countries（发达国家，完成）。
  - 例句：The **exciting** news made us **excited**.（news 令人兴奋用 doing；人感到兴奋用 done）
- 易错：❌ The story is excited. → ✅ The story is **exciting**.（物"令人…"用 doing；人"感到…"用 done）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（议论：内在美）**
- 引入：Nowadays many people care too much about appearance.
- 观点：However, inner beauty, such as kindness and wisdom, lasts longer.
- 结论：We should build up our character rather than only our looks.

**写作框架（议论文：Inner beauty vs appearance，约 100 词）**
- 现象 → 反方观点与反驳 → 结论；用 done 分词短语作定语增色。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（分词作定语）**
- 步骤：名词后有动词且无连词，判断"主动/进行用 doing，被动/完成用 done"。
- 例：The book ______ (write) by the author is popular. → 被写，填 **written**.

**题型2：阅读理解（观点类）**
- 通法：找作者态度词（however, in my opinion）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ The news is excited. → ✅ The news is **exciting**；We are **excited**.
- ❌ a breaking cup（正在打碎的）→ ✅ a **broken** cup（碎了的）。
- ❌ judge by the appearance → ✅ judge by **appearance**（零冠词泛指）。
- ❌ attach importance to do → ✅ attach importance to **doing**.

## Unit 2 一生的事业（A Life's Work，职业与人物）

> 板块：阅读（敬业人物/职业精神）→ 语法（现在分词作定语/状语）→ 写作（人物介绍 + 读后续写）。
> 话题主线：职业选择、敬业精神、榜样人物。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：各行各业、职业精神、劳动价值。
- 功能意念：介绍人物、谈职业理想。
- 语法重点：现在分词 doing 作定语/状语。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| profession | n. | 职业 | a respected profession | Teaching is a noble profession. |
| dedicate | v. | 奉献；致力于 | dedicate…to… | She dedicated her life to teaching. |
| colleague | n. | 同事 | work with colleagues | He gets on well with colleagues. |
| promote | v. | 促进；晋升 | promote development | Exercise promotes health. |
| ambition | n. | 抱负 | achieve one's ambition | His ambition drives him on. |
| skilled | adj. | 熟练的 | be skilled in | She is skilled at design. |
| devote…to | 短语 | 把…奉献给 | to 介词 | He devoted himself to research. |
| be responsible for | 短语 | 对…负责 | Nurses are responsible for patients. |
| look up to | 短语 | 尊敬 | = respect | We look up to hard-working people. |

### 三、语法要点

**现在分词 doing 作定语/状语**
- 通俗：doing 表示"主动、正在进行"。
- 作定语：the **sleeping** boy（= the boy who is sleeping）；The man **standing there** is our teacher.
- 作时间/原因/伴随状语：
  - 时间：**Hearing the news**, she jumped with joy. (= When she heard…)
  - 原因：**Being ill**, he didn't go to school. (= Because he was ill)
  - 伴随：He sat there, **reading a book**.
- 作宾补：I saw him **crossing** the street.（看见他正过马路）
- 易错：❌ Seen from the hill, I love the city.（我"被看"逻辑错）→ ✅ **Seeing** from the hill, I love the city. / **Seen** from the hill, the city looks beautiful.（分词逻辑主语要与句子主语一致）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（人物介绍）**
- 介绍：My role model is a doctor who has devoted himself to rural areas.
- 事迹：Working day and night, he saved many lives.
- 影响：His story inspires me to pursue a meaningful career.

**读后续写（职业/敬业类）细节升级**
- 动作链：With bloodshot eyes, he kept operating without a break.
- 情感升华：It was not a job, but a lifelong responsibility.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（分词作状语）**
- 步骤：逗号后无连词，动词逻辑主语=句子主语；主动用 doing，被动用 done。
- 例：______ (see) from the top, the park is beautiful. → 公园被看，填 **Seen**.

**题型2：读后续写**
- 通法：用现在分词作伴随状语丰富动作（smiling, nodding, wiping tears）。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Seeing from the top, the city is beautiful.（城市不能主动看）→ ✅ **Seen** from the top, the city…
- ❌ dedicate to do → ✅ dedicate…to **doing**.
- ❌ devote his time to help → ✅ devote his time to **helping**.
- ❌ look up to him as a hero（可）注意 to 为介词，后接名词/代词。

## Unit 3 战争与和平（War and Peace，历史与和平）

> 板块：阅读（战争反思）→ 语法（独立主格初步/分词作状语复习）→ 写作（议论文：和平主题）。
> 话题主线：战争的代价、和平的珍贵、历史反思。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会）：历史战争、和平教育、人类命运。
- 功能意念：叙述历史、反思战争。
- 语法重点：分词作状语综合复习 + with 复合结构。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sacrifice | v./n. | 牺牲 | sacrifice one's life | Many soldiers sacrificed their lives. |
| tragedy | n. | 悲剧 | a human tragedy | War is a tragedy. |
| innocent | adj. | 无辜的 | innocent civilians | Innocent people suffered. |
| unite | v. | 团结，联合 | unite against | We should unite against war. |
| mourn | v. | 哀悼 | mourn for the dead | The whole nation mourned. |
| everlasting | adj. | 永恒的 | everlasting peace | We hope for everlasting peace. |
| in memory of | 短语 | 纪念 | = in honour of | The memorial is in memory of heroes. |
| break out | 短语 | 爆发（无被动） | The war broke out in 1939. |
| end up | 短语 | 以…告终 | with 介词 | The talk ended up in failure. |

### 三、语法要点

**with 复合结构（with + 宾语 + 宾补）**
- 通俗：with 短语给句子加一个"附带情景"。
- 结构：with + 名词 + doing（主动）/ done（被动）/ to do（将来）/ 介词短语。
- 例句：
  - He fell asleep **with the light burning**.（灯亮着，主动/进行）
  - She came in **with a book in her hand**.（介词短语）
  - **With the work done**, we went home.（工作被做完）
- 易错：❌ With the problem settling, …（问题被解决）→ ✅ With the problem **settled**…

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（和平反思）**
- 反思：War brings nothing but pain and tears.
- 观点：We must remember history so that peace will last.
- 号召：Let us join hands and say no to war forever.

**写作框架（议论文：Never forget the past，约 100 词）**
- 战争之痛 → 和平之贵 → 青年责任。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（with 复合结构）**
- 步骤：with 后宾语与宾补关系——主动进行 doing，被动完成 done。
- 例：He stood there with his eyes ______ (fix) on the picture. → 被盯住，填 **fixed**.

**题型2：概要写作（历史类）**
- 通法：抓事件 + 教训两句。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ With the work doing, we left. → ✅ With the work **done**.
- ❌ break out 被动 → ✅ 无被动。
- ❌ end up to do → ✅ end up **doing / in + n.**。
- ❌ mourn for 用法：mourn **for/over** the dead。

## Unit 4 未来科技（A Glimpse of the Future，科技发展与创新）

> 板块：阅读（未来科技/AI 伦理）→ 语法（倒装句：否定词开头/only 开头）→ 写作（议论文：科技与人文）。
> 话题主线：AI 未来、科技伦理、人机关系。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与社会/科技）：人工智能、未来生活、伦理思考。
- 功能意念：预测、辩证分析。
- 语法重点：倒装句（部分倒装）。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| artificial | adj. | 人工的 | artificial intelligence | AI is developing rapidly. |
| ethical | adj. | 伦理的 | ethical problems | AI raises ethical questions. |
| predict | v. | 预测 | predict the future | No one can predict the future. |
| replace | v. | 取代 | replace humans | Machines cannot replace human warmth. |
| revolution | n. | 革命 | the digital revolution | Tech has caused a revolution. |
| intelligent | adj. | 智能的 | an intelligent system | The intelligent system helps us. |
| lead to | 短语 | 导致 | to 介词 | Technology leads to changes. |
| depend on | 短语 | 依赖 | = rely on | Don't depend too much on AI. |
| bring about | 短语 | 带来，引起 | = cause | The internet brought about great changes. |

### 三、语法要点

**倒装句（部分倒装）**
- 通俗：把助动词/情态动词提到主语前，像"提问语序"，用来加强语气。
- ① 否定/半否定词开头（never, hardly, seldom, not only, little, not until）：
  - Never **have I seen** such a beautiful place.（正常：I have never seen…）
  - Not only **does he study** hard, but he also helps others.
- ② Only + 状语开头：
  - Only in this way **can we succeed**.
- 易错：❌ Never I have seen… → ✅ Never **have I seen**…
- 易错：❌ Only he can succeed.（only 修饰主语不倒装）；Only **in this way can we** succeed.（only 修饰状语才倒装）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科技伦理）**
- 辩证：While AI brings convenience, it also raises ethical worries.
- 观点：We should use technology wisely, keeping humans in control.
- 升华：Technology is a tool; human kindness is the goal.

**写作框架（议论文：AI—a blessing or a curse，约 100 词）**
- 利 → 弊 → 我的态度（用倒装句提升文采：Only by using it wisely can we…）。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（倒装）**
- 步骤：句首是否定词/only+状语 → 助动词/情态动词提前到主语前。
- 例：Never before ______ (have) I seen such a film. → **have**.

**题型2：书面表达**
- 通法：结尾用 Only in this way can we… 倒装句加分。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ Never I have seen… → ✅ Never **have I seen**…
- ❌ Only by working hard you can succeed. → ✅ Only by working hard **can you** succeed.
- ❌ lead to do → ✅ lead to **doing**.
- ❌ AI 不可数泛指，注意冠词使用。

## Unit 5 向自然学习（Learning from Nature，仿生与自然科学）

> 板块：阅读（仿生学/自然智慧）→ 语法（强调句 It is/was…that…）→ 写作（科普说明文）。
> 话题主线：仿生学、自然启发发明、科学观察。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然）：仿生学、向自然学习、发明创造。
- 功能意念：解释原理、说明灵感来源。
- 语法重点：强调句。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| imitate | v. | 模仿 | imitate nature | Engineers imitate nature. |
| inspire | v. | 启发，激励 | inspire sb. to do | Nature inspires inventors. |
| invent | v. | 发明 | invent a new tool | Humans invented tools. |
| design | v./n. | 设计 | design a structure | The plane is designed like a bird. |
| observe | v. | 观察 | observe carefully | Scientists observe nature. |
| mimic* | v. | 模拟，模仿 | mimic bird flight | The robot mimics insect movement. |
| learn from | 短语 | 向…学习 | We should learn from nature. |
| be based on | 短语 | 基于 | The invention is based on nature. |
| apply…to | 短语 | 把…应用于 | to 介词 | Apply the idea to real life. |

### 三、语法要点

**强调句 It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who…**
- 通俗：想强调句子的某一成分（主语/宾语/状语），就把它放到 It is… that 之间。
- 结构：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that（人可用 who）+ 其余。
- 原句：I met Tom in the park yesterday.
  - 强调主语：It was **I** that/who met Tom in the park yesterday.
  - 强调宾语：It was **Tom** that I met in the park yesterday.
  - 强调状语：It was **in the park** that I met Tom yesterday.
- 判断技巧：去掉 It is…that 后，句子仍完整即为强调句。
- 易错：❌ It was in the park where I met him. → ✅ It was in the park **that** I met him.（强调句用 that，不用 where/when）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**功能句（科普）**
- 灵感：Inventors often get ideas from nature.
- 原理：It was the bird's wings that inspired the design of the plane.
- 意义：Nature is the greatest teacher of mankind.

**写作框架（科普说明文：An invention from nature，约 100 词）**
- 发明是什么 → 灵感来自哪种生物 → 原理与意义；用强调句突出重点。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：语法填空（强调句）**
- 步骤：看到 It is/was… 且去掉后句子完整 → 填 that（人可用 who）。
- 例：It was yesterday ______ I met him. → **that**.

**题型2：概要写作（科普类）**
- 通法：抓"灵感来源 + 发明 + 原理"。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ It was the park where I met him. → ✅ It was **in** the park **that** I met him.
- ❌ apply to do → ✅ apply A **to** B（to 介词）。
- ❌ invent/discover 混：invent 发明原本没有；discover 发现原本存在。
- ❌ inspire sb. doing → ✅ inspire sb. **to do**.

## Unit 6 人与自然综合（综合复习与读后续写，环境与成长）

> 板块：复习本册分词/特殊句式 → 语法（省略句）→ 写作（读后续写综合训练）。
> 话题主线：综合运用、自然与人的关系。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题（人与自然/自我）：综合话题，写作训练为主。
- 功能意念：综合叙述与议论。
- 语法重点：省略句。

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 常见搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| appreciate | v. | 欣赏；感激 | appreciate nature | We should appreciate nature. |
| harmony | n. | 和谐 | in harmony with | Live in harmony with nature. |
| sustainable | adj. | 可持续的 | a sustainable future | Build a sustainable future. |
| precious | adj. | 珍贵的 | precious resources | Water is a precious resource. |
| preserve | v. | 保护，保存 | preserve nature | Preserve the environment. |
| in a word | 短语 | 总之 | = in short | In a word, nature is our home. |
| as a result | 短语 | 结果 | = therefore | As a result, we must act now. |
| from then on | 短语 | 从那时起 | From then on, he changed. |

### 三、语法要点

**省略句**
- 通俗：为避免重复，把句子中能省的成分省掉。
- 状语从句省略：当主从句主语一致（或 it 是），且含 be，可省"主语+be"。
  - 例句：When (you are) in Rome, do as the Romans do.
         Though (he was) tired, he went on working.
  - 例句：If (it is) necessary, call me.
- 不定式省略：I don't want to go there, but I have to (go there).（保留 to，省动词）
- 易错：❌ When asked about it, he kept silent.（此为正确省略：When (he was) asked…）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

**读后续写综合框架（情绪曲线）**
- 第一段：承接原文冲突，用动作+心理推进（With his heart pounding, …）。
- 第二段：冲突解决，情感升华（It dawned on him that… / From then on, …）。
- 高频衔接：To his surprise, … / Without hesitation, … / At that moment, … / Eventually, …

**概要写作综合框架**
- 一句话主题（The passage mainly tells…）+ 两要点 + 一句结论，约 30 词。

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

**题型1：读后续写（综合）**
- 通法：① 通读标人物/情绪/冲突 → ② 两段各 60 词，第一段推情节，第二段点主题 → ③ 检查时态人称一致、衔接语块到位。

**题型2：语法填空（省略/分词）**
- 通法：状语从句中主语与主句一致且含 be，可考虑省略；非谓语作状语注意逻辑主语。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ When seeing from the hill, the city is beautiful. → ✅ When **seen** from the hill, the city…（城市被看）。
- ❌ in harmony to → ✅ in harmony **with**.
- ❌ 读后续写与原文时态不一致（原文过去时，续写用现在时）→ ✅ 统一一般过去时。
- ❌ From then on 后接现在时 → ✅ 常接一般过去时。
