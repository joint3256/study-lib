## Module 1 过去正在发生（Past Continuous）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 过去进行时结构；Unit 2 when/while 区分。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**日常活动与作息（回忆某一时刻）、社会事件**。
- 功能意念：描述"过去某个时间点正在做什么"。
- 语法核心：过去进行时（was/were + doing）。

```
was/were + doing(过去某刻正在做) → at 8 last night → when(点)/while(段)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| rain | v. | 下雨 | It was raining. |
| storm | n. | 暴风雨 | a heavy storm |
| wind | n. | 风 | strong wind |
| light | n. | 光；灯 | the light outside |
| report | v./n. | 报道；报告 | a news report |
| area | n. | 地区 | in this area |
| strange | adj. | 奇怪的 | a strange noise |
| shock | v./n. | 震惊 | be shocked at |
| silence | n. | 沉默；寂静 | in silence |
| recently | adv. | 最近 | until recently* |

- 重点短语：at that time（那时）；at eight o'clock last night（昨晚八点）；pick up（接电话/捡起）；fall asleep（入睡）。

### 三、语法要点

**过去进行时**
- 结构：was/were + 动词-ing。I/He/She/It + was；We/You/They + were。
- 用法：表示过去某一时刻或某段时间"正在进行"的动作。
  - I **was watching** TV at eight last night. / They **were playing** basketball then.
- 标志：at that time, at 8 yesterday morning, at that moment, when/while 引导的从句。
- **when vs while**：
  - when 后可接时间点（短动作，一般过去时）：When the teacher came in, we were talking.
  - while 后接时间段（长动作，常用过去进行时）：While I was reading, my mother was cooking.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What were you doing at eight last night? — I was doing my homework.
  — What were you doing when the rainstorm came? — I was reading in my room.
- 事件回忆框架：
  > Yesterday evening, I was walking home when it began to rain. While I was running to a bus stop, I met my friend. We were waiting for the bus when we saw an accident.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（过去进行时标志）**：步骤——看到"过去具体时刻"用 was/were + doing。
  - 例：What ___ you ___ (do) at nine last night? → **were; doing**。
- **单项选择（when/while）**：步骤——从句动作短用 when，长用 while。
  - 例：___ I was reading, the phone rang. → 长动作，**While**。
- **完形（事件叙事）**：步骤——区分"背景（进行时）"与"插入的短动作（一般过去时）"。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I was do my homework. → ✅ I was **doing** my homework.
- ❌ He were reading. → ✅ He **was** reading.
- ❌ When I walked, he read. → ✅ 强调背景用进行时：While I **was walking**, he **was reading**.
- ❌ at last night（多余介词）→ ✅ last night（不加 at）。
- ❌ What you were doing? → ✅ What **were you** doing?（疑问语序）。

## Module 2 经历与成就（Experiences）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 have/has + 过去分词；Unit 2 have been to vs have gone to。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**旅行与交通（经历）、社会与文化**。
- 功能意念：谈"曾经去过/做过什么"。
- 语法核心：现在完成时入门（have/has + done）。

```
have/has + 过去分词 → 经历(ever/never/been to) → have been to(去过已回) vs have gone to(去了未回)
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ever | adv. | 曾经 | Have you ever been…? |
| never | adv. | 从不；从未 | I have never been… |
| already | adv. | 已经 | I have already finished. |
| yet | adv. | 还（未）；尚 | Have you finished yet? |
| just | adv. | 刚刚 | I have just seen him. |
| been | v. | be 的过去分词 | have been to |
| seen | v. | see 的过去分词 | have seen |
| traveled | v. | travel 的过去分词 | have traveled |
| island | n. | 岛 | a beautiful island |
| fantastic | adj. | 极好的 | a fantastic trip |

- 重点短语：have been to（去过已回）；have gone to（去了未回）；so far（到目前为止）；a couple of（两个/几个）。

### 三、语法要点

**现在完成时入门**
- 结构：have/has + 过去分词。
- 用法1——谈经历："曾经做过"，常与 ever/never 连用。
  - I **have been** to Beijing. / **Have** you **ever** eaten sushi?
- have been to vs have gone to：
  - have been to + 地点：去过（人已回来）。I have been to Shanghai.
  - have gone to + 地点：去了（人还在那里）。He has gone to Shanghai.（他不在这儿）
- 过去分词：规则动词同过去式（worked/visited）；不规则需背（go→gone, see→seen, eat→eaten, be→been）。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Have you ever been to an English-speaking country? — Yes, I have. / No, never.
  — Where has he gone? — He has gone to the library.
- 我的经历框架：
  > I have ever been to many places. I have been to Beijing twice. I have never been to a foreign country. But I have already learned English for three years. I hope to travel abroad one day.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（have been to vs have gone to）**：步骤——人在不在。在→been to；不在→gone to。
  - 例：Where is Tom? — He ___ the library. → 人不在，**has gone to**。
- **单项选择（already/yet）**：肯定句用 already，否定/疑问用 yet。
  - 例：I haven't finished ___. → **yet**。
- **词形变化（过去分词）**：步骤——不规则单独记。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I have been to the library, but I will come back soon.（人已回语境用 been 对；若说他不在则用 gone）→ ✅ 先判断人是否在场。
- ❌ He has went to Beijing. → ✅ He has **gone** to Beijing.
- ❌ I have seen it yesterday. → ✅ 有明确过去时间用一般过去时：I **saw** it yesterday.（现在完成时不与明确过去时间连用）。
- ❌ Have you ever go? → ✅ Have you ever **been/done**?
- ❌ have been in vs have been to 混用 → ✅ have been to + 去过的地点；have been in + 待了多久。

## Module 3 情感与人际交往（Feelings & Friendship）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 宾语从句初步（that）；Unit 2 感叹句。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**情感与人际交往、朋友与周围的人**。
- 功能意念：表达感受、转述他人的话。
- 语法核心：宾语从句初步（that 引导）、What/How 感叹句。

```
I think (that)… → 宾语从句用陈述语序 → What(a/an)+n.! / How+adj./adv.!
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| feel | v. | 感觉 | feel nervous |
| nervous | adj. | 紧张的 | be nervous about |
| shy | adj. | 害羞的 | a shy girl |
| proud | adj. | 自豪的 | be proud of |
| sad | adj. | 难过的 | feel sad |
| excited | adj. | 兴奋的 | be excited about |
| lucky | adj. | 幸运的 | a lucky day |
| friendship | n. | 友谊 | true friendship |
| believe | v. | 相信 | I believe (that)… |
| worry | v./n. | 担心 | worry about |

- 重点短语：be afraid of（害怕）；be good with（与……相处好）；make friends（交朋友）；worry about（担心）。

### 三、语法要点

**1. 宾语从句初步（that 引导）**
- 通俗：把一个句子当作动词的宾语。主句 + that + 从句。
  - I think **(that) he is right**. / She said **(that) she was happy**.
- that 在口语中常省略；从句用**陈述语序**（主语在前，谓语在后）。
  - ❌ I think that is he right. → ✅ I think (that) **he is right**.

**2. 感叹句**
- What + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 (+ 主语+谓语)！
  - **What** a clever boy (he is)! / **What** fine weather (it is)!（weather 不可数不加 a）
- How + 形容词/副词 (+ 主语+谓语)！
  - **How** clever the boy is! / **How** fast he runs!
- 选 what 还是 how：看感叹中心是名词用 what，是形容词/副词用 how。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — I feel nervous because I will give a talk.
  — I believe that you can do it well.
  — What a nice day! / How beautiful she is!
- 安慰朋友框架：
  > I know you feel sad, but don't worry. I believe that you will do better next time. What a good friend you have! Let me help you.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（宾语从句语序）**：步骤——从句必须是陈述语序（主语+谓语）。
  - 例：I don't know ___. → 选 **where he lives**（不选 where does he live）。
- **单项选择（what/how 感叹句）**：步骤——找感叹中心词：名词用 what，形/副用 how。
  - 例：___ fine weather it is! → 中心 weather 名词，**What**（不可数不加 a）。
- **书面表达（分享感受）**：步骤——用 I feel… because… / I think that…

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ I think that is he wrong. → ✅ I think (that) **he is wrong**.
- ❌ What a fine weather! → ✅ What **fine** weather!（weather 不可数，不加 a）。
- ❌ How a clever boy! → ✅ **What** a clever boy! / **How** clever the boy is!
- ❌ I don't know where does he live. → ✅ I don't know where **he lives**.
- ❌ exciting（令人兴奋）与 excited（感到兴奋）混用 → ✅ -ing 修饰事物，-ed 描述人感受。

## Module 4 最高级与推荐（Superlatives & Recommendations）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 形容词/副词最高级；Unit 2 推荐与选择。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**购物、饮食与健康、旅行与交通（推荐）**。
- 功能意念：在多个中"最……"、给出推荐。
- 语法核心：最高级。

```
最高级(-est/most…) + the → A is the + 最高级 + of/in… → 推荐句型 Why not…?
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| best | adj./adv. | good/well 的最高级 | the best |
| worst | adj. | bad 的最高级 | the worst |
| biggest | adj. | big 的最高级 | the biggest |
| most | adv. | many/much 最高级标记 | the most popular |
| popular | adj. | 受欢迎的 | the most popular |
| comfortable | adj. | 舒适的 | the most comfortable |
| cheapest | adj. | cheap 最高级 | the cheapest |
| service | n. | 服务 | good service |
| quality | n. | 质量 | high quality |
| choice | n. | 选择 | a good choice |

- 重点短语：one of + the + 最高级 + 复数名词（最……之一）；the best way to do（做……最好的方法）。

### 三、语法要点

**形容词/副词最高级**
- 构成（在比较级基础上）：
  - 单音节 + est：tall→tallest；e 结尾 + st：nice→nicest；双写：big→biggest；y 变 iest：easy→easiest。
  - 多音节：most + 原级：most popular, most comfortable。
  - 不规则：good/well→best, bad→worst, many/much→most, little→least。
- 句型：the + 最高级 + of/in 范围。
  - Tom is **the tallest in** his class. / This is **the best of** the three.
- one of + the + 最高级 + 复数名词：Beijing is one of **the biggest cities** in China.

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What's the best movie theater? — Town Cinema is. It has the best service.
  — Why not go to the cheapest restaurant?
- 推荐框架：
  > I think Dongfang Restaurant is the best. It has the most delicious food and the friendliest service. It is also one of the cheapest. So it is the best choice for us.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（最高级标志范围）**：步骤——看到 in/of 范围、三者以上用最高级，加 the。
  - 例：He is ___ (tall) in our class. → **the tallest**。
- **单项选择（one of 结构）**：one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。
  - 例：One of the ___ (good) ways… → **best**。
- **阅读（广告推荐）**：步骤——抓 best/cheapest/most comfortable 等比较信息。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ He is tallest in class. → ✅ He is **the** tallest.（最高级前通常加 the）。
- ❌ one of the best student → ✅ one of the best **students**（复数）。
- ❌ most cheap → ✅ **cheapest**。
- ❌ the most more beautiful → ✅ **the most beautiful**。
- ❌ my the best friend → ✅ 物主代词后不加 the：my **best** friend。

## Module 5 动物与保护（Animals & Protection）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 动词不定式；Unit 2 濒危动物保护。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**动物与保护**。
- 功能意念：谈论动物、表达目的与建议。
- 语法核心：动词不定式（to do）初步。

```
want/ decide/ hope to do → It's + adj. + to do → 表目的 to do → 保护动物
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| animal | n. | 动物 | wild animals |
| panda | n. | 熊猫 | live in China |
| tiger | n. | 老虎 | endangered tigers* |
| elephant | n. | 大象 | African elephants |
| endangered | adj. | 濒危的 | endangered animals |
| protect | v. | 保护 | protect pandas |
| nature | n. | 自然 | nature reserves* |
| feed | v. | 喂养 | feed on |
| situation | n. | 情况；处境 | in a difficult situation |
| develop | v. | 发展；开发 | develop quickly |

- 重点短语：in danger（处于危险中）；take away（带走）；live on（以……为食）；think about（考虑）。

### 三、语法要点

**动词不定式 to do 初步**
- 结构：to + 动词原形。
- 作宾语：want/ decide/ hope/ plan/ learn/ agree + to do。
  - I want **to see** pandas. / He decided **to help** them.
- 作主语（常用 it 作形式主语）：It's + 形容词 + to do sth.
  - It's important **to protect** animals. / It's easy **to learn** English.
- 表目的：We should work hard **to save** them.（为了……）。
- 易错：不定式的否定在 to 前加 not：decide **not to give** up。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — Why are pandas endangered? — Because they don't have enough food.
  — We need to protect animals. It's our duty to save them.
- 保护动物框架：
  > Many animals are in danger, such as pandas and tigers. We must do something to protect them. It's important to give them enough food and safe places to live. Let's work together to save animals.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（后接 to do 的动词）**：步骤——want/hope/decide/plan/learn 后接 to do。
  - 例：He hopes ___ (see) the panda. → **to see**。
- **单项选择（It's + adj. + to do）**：步骤——真正主语是后面的 to do。
  - 例：It's important ___ (learn) English well. → **to learn**。
- **书面表达（Save animals）**：步骤——问题→原因→措施→呼吁。

### 六、易错易混

- ❌ He decided helping. → ✅ He decided **to help**.
- ❌ It's important learn. → ✅ It's important **to learn**.
- ❌ want do → ✅ want **to do**.
- ❌ stop to do（停下来去做另一件事）与 stop doing（停止正在做的事）混淆 → ✅ 记：stop to do 是"去做"，stop doing 是"别做"。
- ❌ I want to going. → ✅ I want to **go**.（to 后原形）。

## Module 6 科普常识与技术（Science & Technology）

> Unit 构成：Unit 1 被动语态入门；Unit 2 发明与科技。

### 一、模块地图与话题主线

- 话题主线：**科普常识与技术**。
- 功能意念：描述"东西被……"、介绍发明。
- 语法核心：一般现在时被动语态（am/is/are + done）。

```
宾语变主语 → be + 过去分词 → 一般现在被动 am/is/are + done → by sb.
```

### 二、核心词汇与短语

| 词 | 词性 | 释义 | 搭配/例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| invent | v. | 发明 | invent a machine |
| invention | n. | 发明物 | a great invention |
| computer | n. | 电脑 | on the computer |
| screen | n. | 屏幕 | the screen |
| machine | n. | 机器 | a washing machine |
| science | n. | 科学 | modern science |
| technology | n. | 技术 | high technology* |
| useful | adj. | 有用的 | a useful tool |
| replace | v. | 代替 | be replaced by |
| allow | v. | 允许 | be allowed to do |

- 重点短语：be made of/from（由……制成）；be used for（被用于）；be famous for（因……著名）。

### 三、语法要点

**被动语态入门（一般现在时）**
- 通俗：主语是"动作的承受者"时用被动。主动：People speak English. → 被动：English **is spoken** by people.
- 结构：am/is/are + 过去分词。
  - The classroom **is cleaned** every day. / These computers **are made** in China.
- be made of（看得出原料）/ be made from（看不出原料）：The desk is made **of** wood. / Paper is made **from** wood.
- 否定疑问在 be 上变化。

### 四、话题表达与写作

- 功能句：
  — What is it used for? — It is used for searching information.
  - The telephone was invented in 1876.*（过去被动将在九上深化）
- 介绍发明框架：
  > The computer is one of the most useful inventions. It is widely used in our life. Information is stored in it. Many tasks are done by computers. It makes our life easier.

### 五、常考题型与解题方法

- **单项选择（被动判断）**：步骤——主语是动作承受者（东西被做），用 be + done。
  - 例：The bridge ___ (build) last year.（过去被动，九上深化）→ **was built**。
- **单项选择（be made of/from）**：步骤——看得出原料用 of，看不出用 from。
  - 例：The paper is made ___ wood. → **from**。
- **阅读（科技说明文）**：步骤——抓定义、用途、优缺点。

### 六、易错易混

- ❅ English speaks by many people. → ✅ English **is spoken** by many people.
- ❌ The book written by Lu Xun.（缺 be）→ ✅ The book **is written** by Lu Xun.
- ❌ be made in 与 be made of 混用 → ✅ in + 地点；of/from + 原料。
- ❌ The glasses are made of glass.（看得出原料用 of 对）。
- ❅主动被动混用时态错 → ✅ 先定时态（时间状语），再定主被动，最后写 be + done。
